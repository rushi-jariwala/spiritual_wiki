A close friend of my father's, Dr. Chandra, once visited the Bhrigu Nadi at Hoshiarpur in Punjab to have his family's futures read. His son had just enrolled at a film institute — handsome, talented, pointed at a career that seemed made for the screen. The Bhrigu Nadi said plainly: no material future in films. No career. Nothing. We dismissed it, as one dismisses things that collide with the obvious. The son graduated, bagged a lead role in a well-known film, and stood alongside Dharmendra at the height of his stardom. The film bombed. The roles dried up. The boy, by all worldly measures better looking than most actors of his generation, now teaches song and drama in a school in South India.

*A film star he did not make, and Bhrigu predicted that thousands of years ago.*

*(→ [[stories/bhrigu-dr-chandra|Bhrigu's Prophecy — Dr. Chandra's Family]])*

The same reading covered the rest of the family. Dr. Chandra's daughter was famously shy of men — she had refused every introduction, including a handsome acquaintance I personally sent her way. Bhrigu predicted she would have two children. She married at thirty-nine, a man who already had a son from a prior marriage, and together they had one child of their own. And then Bhrigu said that Dr. Chandra himself would die in a particular August. The man was physically fit. Nobody believed it. He died of natural causes that August. Three separate lives. Three predictions. One sage who had not drawn a breath in thousands of years.

**The Mechanism Behind It**

To understand this, you have to accept that the future has already happened. Not in theory — in fact. Somewhere in a cross-section of time and space that lies ahead of us, the events of our lives are already fixed. *Aatma Sutra* puts the mechanism precisely:

> "Destiny is the result of karmas waiting to fructify. They do so only in alignment with the planetary movements. When the position of the planets is adverse, negative karmas get fructified. And conversely, when there is a positive planetary movement, positive karmas get fructified."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.V, p.51

Bhrigu, working from the planetary configurations of an unborn soul, could read the outline of an entire life because the karmic balance sheet — everything accumulated across lifetimes, stored in the causal body — moves with the planets, not with our intentions. Which is why the pendulum swings as it does: good days, bad days, right calls, wrong turns, one moment you like your life and the next you do not. *Duality has its day. Delusion has its way.* The mistake is to read each swing as news, as a verdict on your character, when it is merely the river following the channel that was cut before you arrived.

**What the Seers Already Knew**

Around thirty years before the Mumbai floods of 2005, Gurudev told us they would come — water levels alarmingly high, many deaths. We heard him with that half-attention we give to extraordinary things said matter-of-factly. Two decades after he passed on, the city flooded.

A college friend of mine — a chartered accountant then, with a small practice and a generous heart — used to come with me to the sthan. Gurudev would tell him with an unhurried certainty: "I will make you a world-famous leader and one of India's admired politicians." The man had no party affiliation. The prediction seemed, to both of us, kindly optimistic at best. He rose to become a cabinet minister, holding several important portfolios, invited internationally to speak, admired for his humility and honesty. He lives simply. *He knows what he is today was exactly what he was meant to be, hence the humility.*

*(→ [[stories/gurudev-predictions-mumbai-politician|Gurudev's Predictions — Mumbai Floods and the Politician]])*

If these men could read the future — Bhrigu, Nostradamus, Gurudev — then the future was there to be read. Think of it like a film that has already been made. You are sitting in the hall with your popcorn, watching the second half, certain that the outcome depends on your anxiety. The director finished the film years ago. Your worry does not rewrite the ending. The seers had simply seen the print.

**The Question This Raises**

Once you accept the pre-mapped nature of destiny, a question becomes unavoidable:

> "If the trends of your life are predetermined and the future has already happened in a cross-section of time and space that lies ahead, how do you examine the concept of guilt?"
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.V, p.60

We are destined to make the mistakes we make, to say foolish things in precisely the chapters we say them, to be selfish in some stretches and sacrificing in others. The karmic configuration of our birth sets the channel. The guilt we pile on top — for the failed career, the broken relationship, the years wasted — is a fiction applied over a script we did not write. This is not license for indifference. It is the removal of a category error.

> "This is what I was meant to be and this is what I am. This is me — the created, the intended, the destined. The result of the karmic me. But not the 'guilty' me."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.V, p.61

**One Nuance Worth Carrying**

Accepting destiny does not mean the guru cannot act. I once rode a scooter to Delhi with Gurudev's driver. Earlier that afternoon, drowsy in the courtyard, I had seen a vision of a cycle cart, a fall, my sleeve torn, my hand bruised and bleeding. When the exact cycle cart appeared at the exact crossing, I knew before the scooter skidded. The sleeve tore. The bruise came. There was no bleeding at all.

*(→ [[stories/scooter-accident-vision|The Scooter Accident Vision]])*

Gurudev accepted my thanks with a wry grin and said nothing. The fall happened precisely as seen. What his grace altered was the intensity of its expression — the bleeding that the vision had shown and that his blessing quietly removed. Destiny minus the bleeding. The karma Sutra calls this the guru's umbrella: the rain still comes, but the disciple walks through it without getting drenched.

**What the Mahaguru Chose**

All of this holds for us as conditioned beings, working within the limits of accumulated karma. At the highest level of evolution, the relationship with destiny inverts. *The Guru of Gurus* records this observation about Gurudev with care:

> "The mahaguru's supernature was not limited by his destiny, but he chose to limit his destiny to express his supernature."
> — *The Guru of Gurus*, p.248

Ordinary beings are shaped by their destiny. Gurudev chose his — not because he had escaped the system, but because at the level he had reached, his will and his karma had become the same thing. The patient queue on Thursday mornings, the servant's life, the commoner's clothes — none of it was a ceiling. It was a precision instrument, chosen from a vantage point of limitlessness to accomplish exactly what needed accomplishing.

We work backward from that: to earn the freedom to choose your destiny, you first have to stop fighting the one you have.
