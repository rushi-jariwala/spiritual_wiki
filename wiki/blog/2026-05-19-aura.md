On one of my out-of-body journeys — it was about four in the morning — I floated above my sleeping wife and, in a moment of irresistible mischief, launched myself at her to startle her awake. Every time I tried, I bounced clean off. She did not stir. She slept on, perfectly composed, while I was thrown back again and again. What had stopped me was a blue conical light extending at least a foot in every direction from her body. Without knowing it, without any conscious effort, she was completely protected.

*(→ [[stories/hingori-wife-aura-protection|Hingori's Wife's Aura — The Blue Conical Light]])*

That is when I first truly understood that the aura is not a theory. It is a field of real, measurable energy — your body's outer boundary — and it decides, before you ever know there is a threat, whether that threat will reach you.

**What the Kirilian Camera Found**

To understand what I had witnessed, it helps to know that the aura is not only a spiritual assertion. The Soviet scientist Semyon Kirilian developed a method of photographing it, and in an Indo-Soviet study at Rishikesh in the early 1980s, sixty thousand people were photographed. The results were precise. Corporate executives and traders registered half an inch of aura depth. Farmers and gardeners measured better. Yogis and philanthropists deeper still. Saints who had given their lives to seva and mantra practice registered the greatest aura of all — up to twelve inches.

> "The aura acts like a protective shield to the body. Most external diseases we contract, first infect the aura and then the body."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.IV, p.40

Which means illness is rarely the first event — it is the second. The aura was already weakened before the body became vulnerable. A strong aura is not decorative. It is your first and most important line of defence.

**The Shakti Ball**

Understanding this changes how you see healing. I recall an incident at the sthan involving Nandlal, a sevadaar who had been coming for years. He was struggling to finalise the tenancy of one of his shops — every agreement fell apart just before completion. He suspected something spiritual was blocking it.

I sat in meditation and asked Gurudev to help. During that meditation I saw him throw a ball of energy — dynamic, translucent white, almost a foot in diameter, *expanding and contracting like a small imploding cloud*. I passed it to Nandlal, and it was absorbed into him. The blockage in the shop cleared. Some days later, sitting beside a stranger on a train who was in pain, Nandlal touched the man on impulse. The pain lifted. Without knowing it, he had become capable of healing.

*(→ [[stories/nandlal-shakti-ball|Nandlal's Shakti Ball — Healing and the Price of Ego]])*

But there is a second part to Nandlal's story. Acting on his own authority — with some pride in this new ability — he splashed energised water into a patient's eyes. The woman had spent years in mantra practice and carried a more developed field than Nandlal's. Because he acted egoistically and not on behalf of the sthan, the energy transferred in the direction of the stronger field. *The receiver of the energy was more powerful than the transmitter.* His right hand was depleted for six months. The lesson is precise: healing done without doer-ship — offered to a higher power, not claimed by the self — does not drain the healer. Healing done in one's own name exposes the healer to every risk of the exchange.

**The Ledger You Cannot See**

Understanding how aura depletes opens an even wider question: what is quietly spending yours right now? Anger burns it rapidly. Sustained hatred drains it slowly and without pause. Every vritti — every anxious projection, every memory looping without resolution, every imagination running on its own energy — spends from the same account. Think of the citt as a mobile phone and every thought as an app running in the background. You are not actively using them, but they are drawing down the battery all the same.

There is also this, which I find both unsettling and clarifying: when you think of someone, a remote Bluetooth-like connection is established. Criticise someone regularly and you begin drawing their qualities into your own field — the mind's attention transfers, regardless of the direction. Which is why the Golden Rule here is not merely motivational:

> "What you meditate upon, that you become. Choose prudently."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.X, p.125

**Your Real Balance Sheet**

All of this — my wife's impenetrable sleeping form, Nandlal's six depleted months, the slow bleed of inattention — points to a single deeper truth, and I want you to sit with it.

During a period of what I can only call spiritual adventurism, I once tried to draw energy from a temple without permission. The resident power responded immediately. A heaviness gathered around my head and I could not remain in the car. I walked to Renuka Lake and prayed to Devi Renuka in desperation. In my inner vision I saw a woman dressed in white, crowned and radiant, who touched my head and lifted the attack in a moment. When I returned to Gurudev's camp and sat quietly at the back, he walked over and confirmed what had happened. *I learned the golden rule that energy was better earned than stolen.*

*(→ [[stories/hingori-renuka-temple-attack|Attacked by the Son, Saved by the Mother]])*

There are methods that genuinely build the aura: mantra vidya, seva, greening places, the feet of a genuine saint touched in reverence, the water sipped by someone spiritually evolved. These are not rituals for the sentimental — they are the practical instruments of a tradition that has always understood the aura as the most important asset a person can accumulate.

> "Wealth across lifetimes is measured in the currency of *shakti*. You are only as rich as your aura and its potential."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.IV, p.49

The property you have built, the reputation you have gathered, the balance in the bank — none of it crosses the border at death. The aura does. It is the only inheritance the spirit body carries forward, and it determines everything that comes after: the comfort, the protection, the freedom, the company you keep in the realms beyond the physical. Most people do not spend a single hour thinking about it. They will have centuries to regret that.

The woman asleep in a blue conical light had earned something real without knowing it. Begin earning it today.
