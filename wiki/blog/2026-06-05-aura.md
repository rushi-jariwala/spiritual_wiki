I remember an evening — or what felt like one — when I found myself out of my body, drifting through the room where my wife was asleep on the floor. It was around four in the morning. I will admit this without dignity: my first thought was to pull a prank. I tried to bounce on her to give her a fright. What happened instead stopped me completely. Every time I approached, I was pushed back. She was encased in a blue conical light, extending at least a foot off her body, and it was impenetrable. She slept on, undisturbed.

*That is when I realised that the human aura is a powerful protective shield.*

*(→ [[stories/hingori-wife-aura-protection|Hingori's Wife's Aura — The Blue Conical Light]])*

I have spent years thinking about that moment. What I saw, and what I could not breach, was not magic. It was the visible expression of what every one of us carries — a bioelectric field of energy, the radiation of *shakti* stored in the spirit body. You are surrounded by it at every moment. You simply cannot see it with the physical eye.

**What the Camera Already Knows**

And this is not a matter of belief. In the early 1980s, Soviet scientist Semyon Kirilian developed a method of photographing the aura, and in an Indo-Soviet experiment at Rishikesh, sixty thousand people were photographed. The results were measured, sorted, and compared. Corporate executives and traders: barely half an inch of aura depth. Farmers and gardeners: rather better. Yogis and philanthropists: deeper still. Saints and dedicated spiritual practitioners: up to twelve inches.

The Kirilian camera was not measuring morality or virtue. It was measuring energy — accumulated *shakti*, the quiet dividend of a life spent in the right direction.

> "Wealth across lifetimes is measured in the currency of *shakti*. You are only as rich as your aura and its potential."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.IV, p.49

This is the thing that changes everything, once you take it seriously: the aura is not a spiritual decoration. It is the only currency that survives death.

**The Invisible Channel**

The aura is not a closed system, and here is where it becomes important to understand the mechanism. When you think of someone — even casually, even critically — a connection is established between you. Think of it as a wireless channel that opens the moment your attention is directed. Criticise someone regularly, and you gradually magnetise some of their qualities. The very traits you find fault with are the ones you risk absorbing. Praise someone consistently, and the same channel carries their finer qualities toward you.

Practitioners of yoga have used this principle deliberately for centuries: meditating on a horse for speed, on an elephant for strength, on a lion for courage. The channel does not discriminate — it simply connects and transfers, indifferent to whether the signal is welcome.

> "What you meditate upon, that you become. Choose prudently."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.X, p.125

**The Shakti Ball — And What Happened After**

Understanding the aura as something that can be given, received, and transmitted is not just philosophy. I once sat in meditation and asked Gurudev to help a devotee named Nandlal, who had a spiritual obstruction blocking a shop he was trying to rent. What I saw during that meditation was remarkable: Gurudev threw a ball of energy at me — dynamic, translucent white, almost a foot in diameter, pulsing and alive, expanding and contracting like a small imploding cloud. I transmitted it to Nandlal. It cleared his problem.

Not long after, Nandlal found himself on a train next to a stranger in pain. He touched the man, and the pain disappeared. He had healed someone without even understanding it, simply because the energy Gurudev had transmitted through him was still at work.

Then came the sharper teaching. Nandlal, emboldened, once used the healing process with ego — splashing energised water into a patient's eyes with pride, claiming the act as his own. The patient happened to be spiritually more advanced. Energy flows toward the stronger field. Nandlal's right hand weakened immediately, and for six months he bore the imbalance of a drained aura.

*(→ [[stories/nandlal-shakti-ball|Nandlal's Shakti Ball — Healing and the Price of Ego]])*

Healers who operate on behalf of a higher power are protected by it. When the ego steps forward and claims the act, it also claims the cost.

**Energy Better Earned Than Stolen**

I have learned this from the other direction too. During a period of spiritual immaturity and adventurism, I once tried to draw energy from a temple in Renuka while at prayer. I felt the attack almost immediately — a destabilisation of mind and body, a heaviness gathering around my head. I knew from past experience that something powerful was responding. I sat in the car, could not settle, walked to Renuka Lake, and — with no other option — prayed to Devi Renuka for protection.

What followed appeared on the inner screen behind my forehead: a beautiful woman in white, adorned with ornaments, a crown on her head, radiant. She touched my head, and the heaviness dissolved at once. When I returned to Gurudev's camp, he walked straight to me and confirmed that I had been punished by Parshuramji for what I had tried to do.

*I learned the golden rule that energy was better earned than stolen.*

*(→ [[stories/hingori-renuka-temple-attack|Hingori at Renuka — Attacked by the Son, Saved by the Mother]])*

**The Account That Travels**

All of this points to a single truth. When the body dies, it stays behind. The mind's accumulation of memories and impressions dissolves over time. But the *shakti* stored in the spirit body — the aura — travels forward. It determines what realms the spirit can access, whether it can move freely, whether it can protect itself from more powerful energies that exist between incarnations.

> "Your aura potency, in a manner of speaking, is your cosmic currency and you need a lot of it in order to be rich. It is one of the only assets that you can carry forward when you die, and to your future incarnations. It is also the armour that protects you from the invasion of other energies, including illnesses and spiritual attacks."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.X, p.126

Less than ten percent of this world's population is aware that they carry an aura. Fewer than one percent know enough about it to act on that knowledge.

That is a remarkable statistic. Most of us are arriving at death having never once considered the only account we will keep.

Build it with intention, guard it with awareness — it is the only fortune that follows you.
