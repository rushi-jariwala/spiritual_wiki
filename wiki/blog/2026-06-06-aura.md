On one of my out-of-body journeys, I found myself in my bedroom at four in the morning. My wife lay on the floor, deep in sleep. My wacky sense of humour — which, it turns out, survives astral projection — compelled me to try bouncing on her to give her a fright. What happened next surprised me more than it would have surprised her. Every time I lunged, I bounced straight back, unable to make contact. She was enveloped in a blue conical light extending at least a foot beyond her body — and she did not stir an inch.

*(→ [[stories/hingori-wife-aura-protection|Hingori's Wife's Aura — The Blue Conical Light]])*

I sat in midair and considered what I had just witnessed. The human aura was not a theory. It was a working shield. And my wife — through whatever quiet, accumulated merit of her living — had built hers to a quality that not even her husband in spirit form could breach.

**What the Aura Is**

That blue conical light is not unique to her. Every living being carries such a field — the external expression of the spirit body's accumulated energy, which this tradition calls *shakti*. *Aatma Sutra* describes it precisely: *"The goal of the spirit body... is the attainment of energy that can be used when it exists purely in its non-physical incarnation. This energy is called shakti and is manifested as the 'aura.'"* (Ch.II, p.15)

We tend to think of wealth in the terms we can count — property, investments, things we can bequeath. But most of it gets left at the door when we die. The aura does not. It travels with the spirit body between incarnations, powers its movement through the realms of existence, and protects it from stronger entities along the way. *"Wealth across lifetimes is measured in the currency of shakti. You are only as rich as your aura and its potential."* *(Aatma Sutra, Ch.IV, p.49)*

This is not a metaphor. Kirilian photographic experiments conducted in Rishikesh in the early 1980s measured aura depths across 60,000 people: corporate executives showed the least — barely half an inch. Farmers and gardeners showed better. Yogis and philanthropists more. Saints and spiritual practitioners measured up to twelve inches. The numbers record what each life has generated in spiritual energy. Unlike a tax return, they cannot be falsified.

**Transmit, Deplete — and the Price of Ego**

Every vritti — every thought wave, imagination, desire, or fear — draws from the aura's reservoir. Think of the mind as a mobile phone, each passing thought as an app running in the background, and the aura as the battery. More apps, more depletion.

I once asked Gurudev during meditation to transmit healing energy to a devotee named Nandlal, whose shops had been obstructed by what felt like a spiritual block. What followed was remarkable: Gurudev threw a ball of energy — *"dynamic, translucent white, almost a foot in diameter and felt alive. It kept expanding and contracting like a small imploding cloud."* I passed it on to Nandlal, and within days the obstruction lifted. He met a stranger in pain on a train, touched him, and the pain vanished. He had become, without understanding it, a healer.

*(→ [[stories/nandlal-shakti-ball|Nandlal's Shakti Ball — Healing and the Price of Ego]])*

The sequel is instructive. Nandlal later tried to heal a patient by splashing energised water into her eyes — not on behalf of the sthan's grace, but from his own enthusiasm. He forgot the first law: energy flows to the stronger field. The woman carried years of powerful mantra practice; her aura outpowered his. For six months Nandlal's hand was drained, the energy transferred outward. He paid for what this tradition calls *doer-ship* — claiming the healing act as his own rather than offering it through the guru's power. Non-doership in healing is not modesty; it is the only protection a healer has against absorbing what he draws out.

**Energy Better Earned Than Stolen**

There is a third lesson, and I confess I had to learn it personally. During a period of spiritual immaturity and adventurism, I attempted to absorb energy from a temple near Renuka belonging to one of Gurudev's disciples. Absorbing from a powerful place without permission is not difficult. It is also not forgiven.

Almost immediately a heaviness settled over my head. Something was destabilising me — an attack from a force I could not see or counter. Near Renuka Lake, I prayed to Devi Renuka — the divine mother of sage Parshuramji — and asked for her protection. What appeared on my inner screen was a figure in white, adorned with ornaments and a crown of extraordinary beauty; she touched my head, and the heaviness lifted at once. When I returned to Gurudev's camp and sat quietly at the back of the room, he walked over without being asked and confirmed: Parshuramji had punished me for my error. *Attacked by the son, saved by the mother.*

*(→ [[stories/hingori-renuka-temple-attack|Hingori at Renuka — Attacked by the Son, Saved by the Mother]])*

I have kept the holy water of Renuka Lake in my place of worship ever since, and I have kept the lesson: *energy is better earned than stolen.* *(Aatma Sutra, Ch.X, p.120)*

**The Bluetooth You Cannot Switch Off**

Earning it, it turns out, is largely a matter of sustained attention. *Aatma Sutra* describes a principle from yogic practice that the modern world has inadvertently given a name: every time you think of a person, a remote Bluetooth-style connection opens, and qualities transfer. Criticise someone consistently and you slowly magnetise the very qualities you dislike in them. Admire someone and their qualities begin to infuse your own field. Meditate on a horse for speed, on an elephant for strength, on a spider for patience — not symbolically but because the transfer is real and literal.

*(→ [[concepts/pratipakshbhavana]])*

*"What you meditate upon, that you become. Choose prudently."* *(Aatma Sutra, Ch.X, p.125)*

What you watch, whom you spend sustained time with, the habitual quality of your thoughts — all of it shapes the field you carry when the body ends. This aura, accumulated over a lifetime, is what sustains your spirit in the realms between lives and protects it in whatever comes next.

*"Your aura potency, in a manner of speaking, is your cosmic currency and you need a lot of it in order to be rich. It is one of the only assets that you can carry forward when you die, and to your future incarnations. It is also the armour that protects you from the invasion of other energies, including illnesses and spiritual attacks."* *(Aatma Sutra, Ch.X, p.126)*

Begin investing now, while the account is still open.
