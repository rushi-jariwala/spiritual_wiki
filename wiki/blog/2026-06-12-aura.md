During one of my night-time out-of-body journeys, I found my wife sleeping on the floor at around four in the morning. My sense of humour, which follows me even when I leave the physical body, took over. I decided to bounce on her and give her a fright. What happened next was one of the clearest lessons I have ever received. *Every time I tried to jump on her, I would bounce right back.* She was enveloped in a blue conical light extending at least a foot from her body, and I could not get through it no matter what I tried. She did not stir. The aura had held without her even knowing it was there.

**The Shield That Holds**

That night taught me something no book had yet put into the body. *Aatma Sutra* later did:

> "The aura acts like a protective shield to the body. Most external diseases we contract, first infect the aura and then the body."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.IV, p.40

This is not metaphor. The aura is a bioelectric energy field, measurable by its depth from the body's surface. In an Indo-Soviet experiment at Rishikesh, sixty thousand people were photographed using Kirilian photography. Saints and spiritual practitioners showed auras extending up to twelve inches; the average corporate executive's barely reached half an inch. The data tells its own story about where energy is built and where it is spent. Most diseases do not jump directly to the body — they breach the outer shield first. A powerful aura means a longer buffer. A depleted one means an open door.

**The Ball of Energy**

The aura is not only a personal shield — it can be received, transmitted, and squandered. I once had a disciple named Nandlal, a loyal sevadaar at the sthan for several years, who came to me with a persistent problem: every deal on one of his shops was collapsing just before it closed. He believed the cause was spiritual. I sat in meditation and asked Gurudev to help. In the vision that followed, *Gurudev threw a ball of energy at me — dynamic, translucent white, almost a foot in diameter, expanding and contracting like a small imploding cloud.* I passed it on to Nandlal. Within days, the shops cleared. On a train shortly after, a stranger near him was complaining of pain. Nandlal touched him and the pain disappeared. He had acquired the capacity to heal without knowing how it had happened.

Emboldened by this, Nandlal attempted to heal a woman's eye problem by splashing energised water directly into her eyes — an accepted technique, but one he performed with unmistakable ego. The woman had practiced powerful mantras for years; her aura was more developed than his. Because he had entered the exchange as the owner of the power rather than as its instrument, the energy moved toward the stronger field. His right hand was drained. Six months passed before it recovered. *(→ [[stories/nandlal-shakti-ball|Nandlal's Shakti Ball — Healing and the Price of Ego]])*

The rule is exact: *shakti* belongs to the source, not the transmitter. When the healer acts on behalf of a higher power — the guru, the sthan — he is protected. When he acts in his own name, the field corrects him.

**What Builds It, What Burns It**

I learned the complementary lesson through my own misadventure. Once, in what I can only describe as a period of spiritual adventurism, I visited a temple and attempted to absorb its energy without permission. The temple's force attacked me at once — a heaviness around the head, a destabilising of body and mind that I could not clear on my own. Devi Renuka's intervention at the lake eventually lifted the attack. *I learned the golden rule: energy was better earned than stolen.*

*(→ [[stories/hingori-renuka-temple-attack|Hingori at Renuka — Attacked by the Son, Saved by the Mother]])*

Aura depletes in more ways than most people track. Anger burns it rapidly. Sustained hatred is a slow leak. Every thought, perception, and emotion is a small withdrawal — imagine the *citt* as a phone, and every passing *vritti* as an app running in the background, drawing down the battery whether you are watching it or not. Sexual exchanges, habitual over-talking, even a handshake with a more powerful aura — all drain the reserve. Seva, mantra vidya, greening places, and healing done without doer-ship are the deposits.

There is one rule that surprises people when they first hear it: whatever you fix sustained attention on, you begin to acquire. When you think of someone with intensity — to criticise them, to admire them, to meditate on their qualities — a connection forms and transfer occurs. A remote Bluetooth, as I once called it: the signal does not care whether it is welcome. Criticising someone regularly instils the very qualities you resent. Meditating on a divine quality, or on an animal that embodies a virtue, draws it toward you. The teaching that follows is plain enough:

> "What you meditate upon, that you become. Choose prudently."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.X, p.125

**Your Final Balance Sheet**

When the body falls away, almost nothing of what we spent a lifetime accumulating crosses the threshold — not the property, not the career, not the reputation. The aura does.

> "Your aura potency, in a manner of speaking, is your cosmic currency and you need a lot of it in order to be rich. It is one of the only assets that you can carry forward when you die, and to your future incarnations. It is also the armour that protects you from the invasion of other energies, including illnesses and spiritual attacks."
> — *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.X, p.126

The spirit body exists between incarnations for far longer than any physical life — potentially centuries, in different realms, needing to protect itself from more powerful forces and to ascend toward higher ones. The aura earned in this life is the capital it travels on. What is burned in anger, vanity, and mindless expenditure of attention is simply unavailable for that journey.

We plan our retirements for twenty, perhaps thirty years. Almost no one plans for the centuries the spirit navigates between births. This life, these specific conditions, this body — they are the window in which the currency can be earned. Start now, while the ledger is still open.
