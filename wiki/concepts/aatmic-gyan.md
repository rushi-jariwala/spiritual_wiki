# Aatmic Gyan

## Definition

Aatmic gyan is spiritual or soul-knowledge, most commonly reflected as **intuitiveness**. It is the ability to connect the unconscious, subconscious, and conscious minds into a unified field of knowing — a state in which the spirit's intelligence becomes accessible to the waking self.

> Aatmic gyan is often reflected as intuitiveness and can be attained in various ways. It is also the ability to connect the unconscious, subconscious and the conscious minds.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.19, p.190

## Sources of Aatmic Gyan

Aatmic gyan may arise from two distinct sources:

1. **Carry-forward from a previous incarnation** — the gyan is already embedded in the kaarna sharir and manifests when planetary timing aligns. The person then moves automatically into a more advanced state of thought and foresight, as if waking into knowledge already earned.

2. **Planetary influence in the current life** — specific planetary transits and configurations can trigger an opening of this intuitive capacity. When the appropriate period arrives, the shift happens naturally and without prior effort.

In both cases, the channel is one's own spirit attempting to communicate with its physical body.

> Often, one's spirit tries to communicate with its physical body as intuition.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.19, p.190

## Obstacles

The primary obstacle to aatmic gyan is the turbulence of ordinary mental life:

> Though most experiences are destined, the flurry of thoughts, feelings and emotions disturb the equanimity of the mind and hinder the tranquillity required to trigger intuitiveness.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.19, p.190

Emotion, reactivity, and constant engagement with the outer world drain the inner stillness needed for the spirit's communication to be heard.

## How to Cultivate It

The practice of **thoughtlessness** — the deliberate quieting of the thought-stream — and **meditation** are the recommended methods for acquiring intuitive capacity. Both work by stilling the mental turbulence that blocks the spirit's signal.

> [!tip] This links directly to the Patanjali framework of *citt-vritti-nirodh* — the cessation of thought-ripples — as the precondition for genuine knowing. See [[concepts/citt-vritti-nirodh]].

## Rarity

Not more than 0.001% of people on this planet are intuitive in a developed sense. Aatmic gyan is rare not because it is intrinsically impossible, but because the conditions required — a clear mind, reduced samskar load, favourable planetary timing, and accumulated positive karma — are seldom all present simultaneously.
