# Food as Energy

## Definition

Food and water are not merely physical substances. Indian philosophy holds that they carry the energetic imprint — the emotions, intentions, and consciousness — of those who grow, prepare, and handle them. The subtle energy encoded in a meal reaches the person who consumes it, influencing thought, speech, and disposition. Gurudev lived and taught this principle through every meal he accepted or declined.

> "For Daddy, the significance of food lay not in its taste or flavour, but in the intention and thoughtfulness behind its preparation."
> — Alka ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.90

## The Cook's Energy in Food

Indian philosophy suggests that a dish prepared by two different individuals will carry distinct energies, affecting those who consume it. A cook's emotions and intentions are transferred into the cooking. This is why temple cooks are meticulously selected to ensure that the food they prepare is charged with positive energy.

*Witnessing Greatness* Ch.7 offers a firsthand illustration: Hingori noticed that after eating meals prepared by Sitaram — a domestic helper at his parents' house who frequently used colourful language — his own manner of speaking began to reflect Sitaram's distinctive style. The cook's subtle frequency had entered through the food.

> [!tip] The inverse holds equally. Food prepared by an evolved cook — suffused with calm, devotion, or sattvic intention — carries those qualities into the one who eats it. This is the spiritual logic behind the *bhog* offered to a deity before a meal: the act of offering transforms the food's energetic signature before it is consumed.

## White Foods and Subtle Energies

Light-coloured foods — white dishes, white sweets — are particularly receptive to subtle energies in their environment, positive or negative. This same sensitivity that makes them powerful vehicles for blessings also makes them susceptible to being charged with harmful intent through reverse mantras and negative rituals.

Hingori records his own experience: before his life-changing encounter with Gurudev, white *pedas* (sweets made from *khoa*, sugar, and cardamom) were charged with negativity through specific rituals and reverse mantras and directed at him. He attributes the development of severe arthritis to this. See [[concepts/spiritual-attacks]].

**Protective measure**: adding spices such as turmeric or black pepper to white or light-coloured foods changes their colour and pre-empts the impact of any potential negative energy in one's environment.

## Karmic Dimension of Food

Gurudev taught that one should not feel compelled to consume food financed by others, to avoid incurring a karmic debt; nor should food be selected solely based on flavour. For him, food was not merely sustenance — it carried spiritual significance, reflecting the emotions and intentions of everyone in the chain from producer to preparer.

> "He often picked food from the previous day, even with fresh options."
> — Bittu ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.90

This was not austerity for its own sake, but the mahaguru's triumph over the sense of taste. Whether new or leftover, simple or elaborate, he accepted food without preference — his spiritual path having overshadowed his physical needs.

> "Gurudev readily accepted any vegetarian food cooked for him, no matter how simple. He never demanded particular dishes. His spiritual path had overshadowed his physical needs."
> — Puran ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.90

For karmic debt from food specifically, see [[concepts/karmic-debt]].

## Water as Energy Medium

Water, often described as the planet's lifeblood, reflects the emotions and intentions of those interacting with it. While retaining its chemical composition, water's molecular structure can change — manifesting its dual potential to heal or harm.

At Gurudev's sthans, *jal* is used for healing. This water is infused with powerful mantras, making it a medium of his spiritual energy. Gurudev also used water in a more personal way: he would sip from a glass and then pass the remainder to someone else, sharing his spiritual energy and qualities with them — sometimes to cure, sometimes to advance their spiritual journey.

> He often gave his disciples mantras by sharing his sipped water with them, saying, "I've transferred 10,000 recitations of this mantra to you."
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.91

This statement signified that he was imparting some of his extensive mantra practice to bolster their spiritual development. See [[concepts/mantra-transfer]] for the three forms of mantra transfer, of which sipped water is the most intimate.

On special days such as Guru Purnima, disciples washed Gurudev's feet with water and then drank this water infused with his spiritual energy — receiving the mahaguru's blessings and deepening their bond with him. See [[concepts/guru-vandana]].

A disturbing counter-example: a young girl named Kamlesh from Gurgaon unknowingly drank sherbet mixed with a harmful *taweez*. The consequences would have been dire if the mahaguru had not intervened in time. See [[stories/kamlesh-black-magic-cure]].

## The Feeding-Others Principle

Gurudev extended the teaching about food into the highest register of seva. He often refrained from eating as a gesture of empathy for the less fortunate, and saw the joy of feeding others as superseding the pleasure of eating oneself.

> "Jennu khellaan che mazaa aa gaya na, khaane da mazaa nahin rehnda."
> (When one experiences the joy of feeding others, the pleasure of feeding oneself pales in comparison.)
> — Gurudev, recalled by Renu ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.91

This sentiment — first voiced to Indu Sharma ji, a central figure in the Gurgaon kitchen — is one of his clearest compressed statements on the relationship between food and seva. See [[stories/gurudev-not-eating-hungry-disciples]] for the full account of how this teaching manifested in his daily life.

## In Practice

- **Do not eat food financed by others indiscriminately** — karmic debt accrues regardless of intention.
- **Notice who cooks your food** — the cook's emotional state reaches you.
- **Add turmeric or black pepper to white foods** when consuming them in an environment of uncertain energy.
- **Use water consciously** — it absorbs the intention of whatever is directed at it; bless or energise it before use.
