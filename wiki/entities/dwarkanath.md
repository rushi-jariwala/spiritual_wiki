# Dwarkanath Ji

## Who / What

Dwarkanath ji was a long-time friend of KL Nagpal (Naaga) from their early days in Agra. Upon relocating to Delhi, the two became roommates. Dwarkanath served as the landlord and roommate of Nagpal, and subsequently of Gurudev (the [[entities/mahaguru|Mahaguru]]) when Gurudev moved in with them in East Delhi. Initially oblivious to Gurudev's spiritual powers, Dwarkanath witnessed many mysterious events during their roommate years. He later transitioned from a close friend into an ardent devotee of the Mahaguru.

## Notable Stories

- [[stories/gurudev-delhi-roommates]] — Dwarkanath recalls Gurudev's humility, intellect, extensive travels as a soil surveyor, and the charisma that transformed casual acquaintances into admirers.
- [[stories/gurudev-roommate-mysteries]] — Recalls Gurudev rise in the middle of the night to do *paath* under a blanket, his accurate predictions, and unexplained conveniences like receiving movie tickets without wait.
- [[stories/gurudev-friend-to-devotee]] — Dwarkanath shares his challenging but beautiful experience of visiting Gurgaon and reconciling his memories of his roommate with his new role as a commanding Mahaguru.
