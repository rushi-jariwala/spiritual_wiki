# KL Nagpal

## Who / What

KL Nagpal, affectionately known as **Naaga**, was one of Gurudev's (the [[entities/mahaguru|Mahaguru]]) earliest friends and colleagues at his soil surveying job. Originally from Agra, Nagpal moved to Delhi and shared an apartment with his friend Dwarkanath. Due to a clerical mix-up where he was mistaken for a politician's son of the same name, he landed a government job where he befriended Gurudev, eventually introducing him as a roommate. Nagpal became one of the key figures who supported Gurudev's spiritual mission and later transitioned into a devoted disciple.
