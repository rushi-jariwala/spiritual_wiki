# Santlal Ji

## Who / What

Santlal ji is one of Gurudev's disciples whose path to the mahaguru is described as a transformation "from bureaucratic arrogance to spiritual enlightenment." His story illustrates the teaching that the guru selects the disciple — across nine lifetimes, not just one.

He is mentioned in the *Guru of Gurus* Who's Who, where Gurudev is noted as having seen the trishul symbol on Santlal ji's hand.

## Key Teachings

### The Guru Selects the Disciple

At their first meeting, Gurudev told Santlal ji that the journey to finding a guru is not driven by the disciple's search but by the **guru's intuitive selection** of his disciples. Santlal ji's five-day vigil at the Gangowal sadhu's door — and the sadhu's subsequent admission that Santlal ji's allegiance lay elsewhere — was Gurudev's way of keeping him from the wrong door until the right one opened.

### Nine Previous Incarnations

Gurudev revealed to Santlal ji that the bond between them extended across **nine previous incarnations** — making their encounter in this lifetime not a beginning but a resumption.

### A Mother's Blessings Shift Destiny

Gurudev also revealed that the blessings bestowed upon Santlal ji by his mother had advanced their destined encounter by **two years**. This is one of the rare moments in the corpus where a third person's intervention modifies the timing (though not the substance) of a destined meeting.

> [!tip] This detail sits in subtle tension with destiny as fully fixed. Hingori does not resolve it — the mother's blessings appear to operate *within* destiny, shifting timing but not the fact of the meeting. See the Contradictions section of [[sources/witnessing-greatness]].
