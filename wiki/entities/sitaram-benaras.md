# Sitaram Ji of Benaras

## Who / What

Sitaram ji of Benaras was a highly realized spiritual figure who served as the guru to Sitaram ji of Dasua. Originally residing in Haridwar, he later joined his disciple at the Sheetla Mata temple near Gurudev's house in Punjab. Together with his disciple, he mentored young Gurudev (the [[entities/mahaguru|Mahaguru]]), guiding him to acquire early siddhis and training him in mental focus.

## Key Teachings

Sitaram ji of Benaras brought deep spiritual authority from Haridwar/Benaras, teaching:
- **Siddhi Acquisition & Mastery** — providing the formulas and guidance for gaining spiritual capabilities (siddhis) to heal and help others.
- **Structured Sadhana** — establishing a disciplined, focused path of practices to build resilience and concentration.

## Notable Stories
