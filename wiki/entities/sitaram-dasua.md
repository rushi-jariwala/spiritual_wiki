# Sitaram Ji of Dasua

## Who / What

Sitaram ji of Dasua was an early spiritual mentor to Gurudev (the [[entities/mahaguru|Mahaguru]]) during his youth in Punjab. He lived and practiced at the Sheetla Mata temple located near Gurudev's family house in Hariana. Along with his own guru, Sitaram ji of Benaras, he guided young Gurudev in his initial spiritual pursuits and helped him acquire various siddhis.

## Key Teachings

Sitaram ji of Dasua provided Gurudev with a structured approach to spiritual practices, including:
- **Acquiring Siddhis** — training to harness spiritual power for practical purposes, such as healing diseases and granting wishes.
- **Mental Discipline** — building focus, perseverance, and resilience as foundational traits for spiritual work.
