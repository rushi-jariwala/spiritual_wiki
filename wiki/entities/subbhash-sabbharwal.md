# Subbhash Sabbharwal

## Who / What

Subbhash Sabbharwal ji was a childhood friend of Gurudev (the [[entities/mahaguru|Mahaguru]]) who grew up with him in the town of Hariana, Punjab. After high school, their paths diverged as Gurudev moved to Delhi, but they were eventually reunited after Subbhash learned of Gurudev's spiritual evolution. He sought and received spiritual direction, ultimately becoming an integral member of the administrative team at Gurudev's sthan in Gurgaon.

## Notable Stories

- [[stories/gurudev-dargah-bers]] — Recalls childhood adventures with young Gurudev, including getting into mischief in nearby fields and plucking bers from trees at the school-way dargah.
- **Academics and Reunion** — Recalls that Gurudev never had a keen interest in academics and notes their amusement at not being at the top of their class. Dwelt on the transition of their childhood relationship into a guru-disciple dynamic while keeping the childhood bond intact.
