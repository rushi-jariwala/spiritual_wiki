# Karma Quest

## What It Is

The Karma Quest™ is described as the world's first spiritual IQ test — a diagnostic tool that assesses a person's current stage of spiritual development and offers a personalised prescription for advancement.

> The Karma Quest™ is the world's first spiritual IQ test that offers a personalised prescription for spiritual advancement.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.19, p.199

It is available free of charge at `www.hingorisutras.com` and exists in multiple languages.

## The Four Levels

The prescription categorises a person into one of four ascending levels:

1. **Initiate** — at the threshold of spiritual inquiry
2. **Inclined** — interested but not yet active
3. **Involved** — engaged in regular practice
4. **Intense** — deeply committed to the path

## The 3P Formula

Each level comes with a unique package of advice based on the **3P formula**:
- **Philosophy** — the ideas and understanding appropriate to the level
- **Practice** — the sadhana and seva suited to where one stands
- **Philanthropy** — the form of service that amplifies karmic momentum at each level

## Purpose

Hingori recommends the Karma Quest as a shortcut alternative to maintaining a full **spiritual report card** — a daily personal log of one's activities and attitudes for tracking spiritual growth. The Karma Quest, taken at periodic intervals, provides an external benchmark to complement this internal audit.

> A 'spiritual report card' is a daily log of your activities and can be a self-improvement tool by which you track your spiritual growth... It can also help you view yourself in a third person perspective, thereby being an unemotional and objective observer of your own spiritual journey.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.19, p.197
