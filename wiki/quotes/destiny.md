# Quotes on Destiny

## The Mechanism

> "Destiny is the result of *karmas* waiting to fructify. They do so only in alignment with the planetary movements. When the position of the planets is adverse, negative *karmas* get fructified. And conversely, when there is a positive planetary movement, positive *karmas* get fructified."
> — Hingori, *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.V, p.51

> "And so, oscillates the pendulum — good, bad, right, wrong. One moment you like your life, another moment you do not. Duality has its day. Delusion has its way!"
> — Hingori, *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.V, p.51 [[concepts/destiny]]

## On Astrology

> "As I have always maintained, astrology is an exact science practiced by inexact practitioners."

## Destiny and Free Will

> "Events in life cannot be predicted through logic. We are destined to mess up, say stupid things, be selfish, indifferent, or insensitive or on the other hand, become sacrificing in nature and turn into Gandhi or Martin Luther King or Guru Gobind Singh."
> — Hingori, *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.V, p.60 [[concepts/destiny]]

## Acceptance of Destiny

> "How many of you agree that the greatest obstacle in your life is you?"

## Guru by Destiny (*Guru Sutra*, Ch.16)

> "Neither a guru nor a disciple can be mere coincidences. They are a part of continuity, born to a plan, with a coinciding destiny to support the strategy of this life."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.16, p.180

> — John O'Donohue, quoted in *Guru Sutra*, Ch.16, p.175
