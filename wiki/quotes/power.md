# Quotes — Power

Quotes relating to spiritual power: its nature, transmission, misuse, and protection. Drawn from *Guru Sutra* Ch.13.

---

> "Power, as referred here, is the ability to do things uncommonly."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.127

---

> "Many a siddh guru can awaken the power of knowledge or intuition in his disciples by using his will power or spiritual energy."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.128

---

> "He the 'server', had all of us — the 'laptops' — connected, and hence power became shareable. Naturally, he has access to all the power of every connected device. That is one of the foundations of his finally attaining omnipresence."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.151
> *[[entities/mahaguru]] — the server-laptop metaphor for Gurudev's network of connected disciples*

---

> "An insight Gurudev specifically ingrained in us was that power is not a tool to be used for the benefit of family and friends, or ever misused. However, the use of power for self-protection and sustenance is justifiable."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.138

---

> "Power corrupts, absolute power corrupts absolutely."
> — Lord Acton (British politician), quoted in *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.143
> *Hingori's gloss: corruption by power happens primarily due to a person's insecurities and lack of confidence.*

---

> "I would like to clarify that every person can direct his energy in any direction he pleases, but since most humans have just enough electromagnetic energy to sustain themselves, misusing it is barely possible."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.145

---

> "Envy is a quality that can transcend into the bloodstream, leading a spiritualist to often resort to malpractice by using power to attack he envies."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.139
> *See [[concepts/spiritual-attacks]]*

---

> "Power exists everywhere and there are certain places where power gets accumulated. These places could be busy crossroads, forests, gymnasiums, cowsheds, places of worship, and so on."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.145
> *See [[concepts/gaddi]] — the guru's seat as one such accumulation point*

---

> "It's not a gilded throne that seats the vain. / It's not for those consumed with seeking fame. / It belongs to the energy that selflessly serves / man, animal and spirit in the revered guru's name."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.136
> *See [[stories/guru-sutra-ch13-poem1]]*

---

> "Envy bludgeons reason / with the sight of another on the rise. / Resentment thrives, swords are drawn / so he doesn't reach the prize."
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.140
> *See [[stories/guru-sutra-ch13-poem2]]*
