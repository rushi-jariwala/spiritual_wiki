# Gurudev Appears at the Wedding and in Oslo After His Death

**Source:** *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3 — Impossible Is Possible, p.41–42

## Full Narrative

> Asha Sekhri ji, a long-time devotee, shares: "My daughter called Gurudev on her wedding day and said, 'Gurudev, I will not proceed with the ceremony if you don't attend the wedding.' Gurudev declined as a huge crowd would be at the wedding venue, but my daughter stood her ground. On the night of her wedding, I went upstairs to call her when the baraat arrived. She said, 'Mom, Gurudev had come to meet me.' I was stunned. People were at every gate, and no one had seen him enter the venue! He gifted her his ring, assuring her of his presence whenever she looked at it."
>
> "After her marriage, my daughter relocated to Oslo, Norway. She also began attending a class to learn the local language. The area she lived in was quite isolated. One evening, as she was returning from her class, three men started following her, leaving her quite scared. She remembered the ring Gurudev had gifted her on her wedding day and silently sought his protection. Suddenly, the sound of footsteps intensified behind her. Turning in apprehension, she saw Gurudev, even though he had passed away, walking protectively between her and the men. He accompanied her all the way home and disappeared the moment she opened her front door!"
> — Asha Sekhri ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.41–42

## Context

Both episodes occur after Gurudev's physical passing in 1991 — confirming the tradition's repeated assertion that his death was a change of state, not a cessation of presence. The wedding appearance is particularly noteworthy: the daughter's demand, Gurudev's initial refusal, her insistence — and his arrival through a crowded venue without being seen at any gate. He left a physical token: his ring, charged as a protective presence and reportedly effective as such years later in Oslo.

The ring acts as a *dharmic guarantee* — a commitment underwritten by the mahaguru's continuing consciousness. The Oslo protection is its fulfilment: the daughter's silent appeal triggered the ring's promise, and Gurudev appeared to walk her home. The moment the door opened — safety achieved — he vanished. This precision and economy are characteristic of his post-mortem appearances in *Witnessing Greatness*.
