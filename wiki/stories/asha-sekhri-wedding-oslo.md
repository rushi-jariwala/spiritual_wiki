# Asha Sekhri Ji — Wedding Visit and Oslo Protection

**Source:** *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3 — Impossible Is Possible, pp.41–43

## Full Narrative

Asha Sekhri ji is a long-time devotee. Her account spans two episodes, both occurring after Gurudev's passing in 1991.

### The Wedding Visit

> "My daughter called Gurudev on her wedding day and said, 'Gurudev, I will not proceed with the ceremony if you don't attend the wedding'. Gurudev declined as a huge crowd would be at the wedding venue, but my daughter stood her ground. On the night of her wedding, I went upstairs to call her when the baraat arrived. She said, 'Mom, Gurudev had come to meet me'. I was stunned. People were at every gate, and no one had seen him enter the venue! He gifted her his ring, assuring her of his presence whenever she looked at it."

### Oslo Protection

> "After her marriage, my daughter relocated to Oslo, Norway. She also began attending a class to learn the local language. The area she lived in was quite isolated. One evening, as she was returning from her class, three men started following her, leaving her quite scared. She remembered the ring Gurudev had gifted her on her wedding day and silently sought his protection. Suddenly, the sound of footsteps intensified behind her. Turning in apprehension, she saw Gurudev, even though he had passed away, walking protectively between her and the men. He accompanied her all the way home and disappeared the moment she opened her front door!"

— *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, pp.41–43

## Context

Both events occur after Gurudev's death in 1991 — the wedding visit explicitly so (Asha ji refers to Gurudev declining due to the crowd, then appearing unseen), and the Oslo episode more starkly: Asha ji notes that her daughter "saw Gurudev, even though he had passed away."

The ring gifted at the wedding functions as both a physical token and a live channel: the daughter is instructed to look at it when she needs his presence, and that mental invocation (on the Oslo street) is what activates the protective appearance. The gift is simultaneously a material object and a standing invitation.

The Oslo episode is notable for its specificity: a post-death, physically walked escort on a street in Norway, witnessed only by the person in distress. The protection ends the instant the threat is gone (the moment the door opened).

> [!tip] The ring as an object imbued with consciousness is a precursor to the larger Asthal temple phenomenon — consecrated objects as channels for the mahaguru's living presence. See [[stories/asthal-temple-statues]].
