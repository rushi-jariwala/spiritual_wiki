# Ashish Khanna Ji Brings Water to a Barren Field

## Full Narrative

> Ashish ji shares a memorable event: "A troubled farmer came to the sthan in Gopiganj, deeply concerned about the aridness of his fields. I suggested exploring options such as hand pumps or advanced irrigation methods. Yet, the farmer explained that such efforts were pointless because his land lacked underground water sources, rendering it barren and unable to yield crops."
>
> Determined to help, Ashish ji made his way to the parched land. He began chanting the Mahagayatri mantra and scattered mustard seeds across the terrain. Astonishingly, on his third attempt, water began to surface. Further exploration revealed a hidden underground water source.
>
> Feeling thankful, the farmer asked Ashish ji how he could express his gratitude. Ashish ji advised him to share the water they had discovered with the neighbouring farmers. Taking this counsel to heart, the farmer now shares the water, utilising it for his fields while also assisting his neighbours.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11, pp.155–156

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11 (Sacred Syllables), pp.155–156. Photograph caption: "Water surfaces on arid land after Ashish ji scatters blessed mustard seeds."

## Notes

- Mustard seeds charged with the Mahagayatri appear again in the same chapter at the Durgapur mines ([[stories/kishanmohan-durgapur-mines]]).
- The farmer's thanks is turned into sharing with neighbours — the reward redirected as seva ([[practices/seva]]).
- The chapter closes on a photograph captioned "Gurudev directs the creation of a water canal in Renuka" (p.162).
