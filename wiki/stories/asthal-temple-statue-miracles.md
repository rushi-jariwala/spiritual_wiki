# The Asthal Temple — Establishment and Living Statues

**Source:** *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3 — Impossible Is Possible, p.45–48

## Full Narrative

### Establishment

> A story unfolds in the village of Asthal in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, at a site which was known for an unfinished Shivalay that had not been touched in 16 years. Gurudev appeared in a disciple's dream, asking for its completion, leading to the establishment of a temple and sthan there.
>
> Karamjit ji, a devotee closely linked to this tale, shares a vision he had 35 years ago: "Gurudev showed me a place in Dehradun that would draw and help many people." His surprise grew when he visited Asthal for the temple's inauguration. "The newly established sthan and temple were quite similar to the place Gurudev had shown me in a vision three decades earlier!"
>
> The temple at Asthal has transformed into a powerful site, home to statues of Gurudev and Mataji which are associated with many mystifying events. The creation and consecration of these statues were substantial undertakings. Over a dozen disciples of Gurudev were involved in overseeing their making. More than 65 disciples and their downlines performed the pran pratishtha — a ritual vital to infusing consciousness into the statues. This collective effort has amplified the temple's spiritual potency.

### Ridhim Thapar Ji — The Smiling Statue

> Ridhim Thapar ji, who does healing seva at Asthal, shares, "Once, while praying at the temple, I looked up at the statue. Gurudev's face had turned, and he was smiling at me. It was as though the statue had come to life."
> — Ridhim Thapar ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.46

### Alpana Ji — Comfort, Employment, and Lord Shankar

> Alpana ji, a devotee of Gurudev, reflects on a particularly difficult period in her life: "I was facing the worst financial crisis ever. During that time, I often visited Asthal, sitting in the temple and confiding in Gurudev's statue as though it were my father." She recalls a moment of connection, "Once, as I spoke to the statue, I could have sworn it smiled at me!"
>
> "When my son lost his job in Canada, I implored Gurudev's statue to help him find employment. It felt as though Gurudev was truly listening to me. The very next morning, my son called to tell me, 'Mumma, I've been contacted by Hilton for an interview.' And, miraculously, he secured the job!" Her voice fills with wonder as she adds, "On another occasion, it wasn't Gurudev I saw, but Lord Shankar, sitting in meditation with folded hands, radiating light from where the statue stands. The statue took on a blue hue, glowing with an aura. I was sceptical at first, but each time I looked, I witnessed the same form."
> — Alpana ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.46–47

### Shilpa Malhotra Ji — The Towering Form and the Ball of Light

> Shilpa Malhotra ji, who has dedicated decades to seva, recounts her experience of grace standing in prayer before Gurudev's statue. "In my mind's eye, I saw Gurudev appear in a towering form behind the statue. He looked down at me and extended his hand, offering me a ball of light." When she shared this vision with her guru, he explained that the vision symbolised the transfer of the mahaguru's energy to Shilpa ji.
> — Shilpa Malhotra ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.47

### Mohan the Pandit — From Sceptic to Believer

> Mohan, the temple's pandit, evolved from scepticism to belief through his experiences with Gurudev's statue. "Initially, I doubted Gurudev's powers," he confesses. "However, a visitor encouraged me, saying, 'Don't just see this as a statue; consider it as Gurudev's presence.' I decided to test this belief. Plagued by a toothache, I addressed the statue, requesting healing. I placed a glass of jal at its feet, which I drank shortly after. To my amazement, my toothache disappeared. I later sought relief from some ongoing urinary tract-related problems. Incredibly, that issue was also resolved, and I remained symptom-free for the next two to three years!"
> — Mohan (temple pandit), *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.47

### Gautam Bhushan Ji — Consciousness Inside the Statue

> Gautam Bhushan ji, who conducts healing seva at the Delhi sthan, recounts another compelling experience: "While meditating at the temple at Asthal, I experienced a powerful surge of energy that seemed to transport my consciousness inside Gurudev's statue. Looking through the statue's eyes, I felt an overwhelming sensation of omnipotence, as if I understood everything there was to know. During this state, I saw a devotee from Delhi meditating in the temple. He had been suffering from severe pain and was part of a remote healing group where sevadaars offer healing via video conferencing. I directed my intention toward healing him. The entire experience lasted about 20 minutes."
>
> "About a week later, during a video healing session, the same devotee from Delhi informed us that the intense pain, which he had previously described as '20 on a scale of 10', had completely vanished!"
> — Gautam Bhushan ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.47–48

## Context

The Asthal temple is remarkable for being established through a convergence of *three* independent channels: Gurudev's dream appearance to a disciple asking for the temple's completion; Karamjit ji's vision of the site 35 years prior; and the collective spiritual investment of over 65 disciples performing pran pratishtha. This multi-channel convergence is what Hingori calls the amplification of spiritual potency.

Pran pratishtha — literally "the establishment of the life force" — is the Vedic ritual through which consciousness is ceremonially invited into a statue. The *Witnessing Greatness* accounts suggest the statues at Asthal are not symbolic but functionally charged: a healing interface, a two-way channel, and in Gautam Bhushan ji's case, a portal through which a disciple's consciousness could briefly inhabit the mahaguru's form.

Hingori's closing note in the chapter is characteristic understatement: "The mahaguru's statue has made my role almost redundant, and it is an honour to witness and embrace this irrelevance."
