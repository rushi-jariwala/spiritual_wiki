# Bajrang's Dead Offspring — Gurudev Prays for a Human Birth

## Full Narrative

> Pehalwanji, responsible for caring for Gurudev's Khandsa farm, was a model of devotion. His simple lifestyle belied a loyalty that never wavered. He shares a story about Bajrang, a monkey who lived on the farm. Gurudev had even found a mate for him. When their young one passed, Gurudev retreated into paath. Afterwards, Ram Nivas, who worked at the farm, asked if Gurudev had been praying for the monkey. Gurudev responded, "Yes. I hope to grant Bajrang's dead offspring a future life as a human to repay him for the joy he brought us."
>
> The idea of bestowing a monkey with a human incarnation involves transcending multiple cycles of existence, known as yonis. The concept of reincarnating animals as humans might challenge our conventional understanding of nature, but in the realm of saints, nature herself bends to their rules.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, pp.70–71
