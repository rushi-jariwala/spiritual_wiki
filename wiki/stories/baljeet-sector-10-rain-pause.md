# Baljeet Ji — Nine Days Without Rain at the Sector-10 Sthan

## Full Narrative

> Baljeet ji's pivotal role in constructing the sthans at Najafgarh and Sector-10 in Gurgaon highlights his deep-seated commitment to Gurudev's mission.
>
> Recalling a significant moment from 1989, during the early stages of construction at the sthan at Sector-10, he shares, "It was an overcast day, and I was deeply concerned about possible delays due to the rain. Noticing my unrest, Gurudev approached me. I requested Gurudev for a brief pause in the rain so that our work could continue smoothly."
>
> Gurudev's intervention was truly miraculous. "The rain stopped instantly and did not return for nine days," Baljeet ji says. "Even on the tenth day, as we were installing the lintel, rain soaked the areas around, but our site stayed completely dry."
>
> He reflects on Gurudev's words: "When I thanked him for his intervention, he said, 'Controlling the weather is a simple task for me.'"
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, pp.165–166

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony), pp.165–166.
