# Bharat Dalal ji's Dream — "Seva Never Stops. It Continues 24/7."

## Full Narrative

> Bharat Dalal ji, a sevadaar at the Mumbai sthan, received a pivotal message in a dream from the mahaguru. He recounts, "Two individuals led me to a barren expanse that felt like a different dimension. There, I beheld a striking scene—thousands seated in neat rows before Gurudev. Despite Gurudev speaking in an unfamiliar language, the essence of his words about seva and karma resonated with me intuitively."
>
> As Gurudev moved among the assembled crowd, offering blessings, the moment for Bharat ji's interaction arrived. He recalls, "Gurudev gazed at me with deep intensity. His words were clear and resonant, 'Seva never stops. It continues 24/7.'"
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, pp.60–61

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, "Seva," pp.60–61
