# Devraj Khare Ji — His Mother's Choice

## Full Narrative

> Devraj Khare ji's deep-rooted belief in the sthan can be traced back to his childhood. By the age of six, he was struggling with severe asthma. Around the same time, his mother was diagnosed with leukaemia. In search of healing, both were drawn to the sthan in Mumbai, where they met a disciple of Gurudev.
>
> "Many years later, my mother revealed something to me that I will never forget", Devraj ji shares. "During her first meeting with Guruji, she was presented with a heart-wrenching choice: Guruji could heal only one of us. Without hesitation, she chose me."
>
> "After we had visited the sthan, my health started improving. Yet the true miracle was in my mother's sacrifice," he continues. "Her selfless choice unleashed a flow of blessings. She had leukaemia but lived for almost 18 more years without needing specific treatment for the disease. She had always warned me that her health might worsen, but that day never came. Her haematologist assured her she would not die from cancer. I firmly believe this was due to her selflessness and Guruji's grace."
> — Hingori and Devraj Khare ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.135–136
