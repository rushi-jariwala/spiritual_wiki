# Dhruv Bhalla ji — The Girl Who Could Not Stand

## Full Narrative

> Dhruv Bhalla ji and his wife Kanika ji have dedicated themselves to selflessly serving people in Kolkata, West Bengal. Their work includes performing healing seva and operating a free clinic for pain management.
>
> Dhruv ji shares an insightful story: "A young girl with severe neurological issues had been in our care since she was three years old. Her legs were bent from the hips down, and while she could sit, standing was an impossibility. We diligently worked on healing her for over three years but saw little improvement.
>
> It was a situation that left us all feeling helpless. Despite the absence of progress, her father's unwavering faith in the sthan was particularly moving."
>
> "Then, something extraordinary happened," he explains. "During one of our healing sessions, I heard an inner voice that confidently declared, 'This girl will stand. It will happen.' I hadn't planned to speak those words out loud but I did. The room fell silent, and everyone looked at me as if I had lost touch with reality. That day, the father and daughter left filled with renewed hope, while I was overwhelmed with trepidation."
>
> He shares a pivotal moment: "In the middle of the week, I listened to a segment from Gurudev's audio biography on a podcast, where a story of Gurudev healing someone with a similar problem was featured. The following Saturday, I mimicked Gurudev's method: I positioned my feet near the girl's toes and carefully lifted her by the armpits. Normally, during healing sessions, I would chant a specific mantra, but I intuitively switched to a different one on this occasion. As I held her in that position for a few minutes, I witnessed something extraordinary—her legs began to straighten, and she could stand. She can now stand with assistance. This experience reaffirmed my belief that my role as a healer is to act as a channel for the sthan's energy and fully embrace its guiding flow."
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, pp.65–66
