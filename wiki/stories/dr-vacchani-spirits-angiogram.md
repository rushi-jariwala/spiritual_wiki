# Dr Mahesh Vacchani — Spirits Medicine Cannot See

## Full Narrative

> Dr Mahesh Vacchani, a sevadaar at the Mumbai sthan, opines that sometimes illnesses are hard to understand or treat with conventional medicine because they may be caused by what we cannot see, like spirits or negative energy. Though a firm believer in modern medicine, Dr Vacchani remarks, "A significant proportion of problems are psychosomatic. I once believed spirits were folklore, but the sthan has clarified my understanding."
>
> He has integrated conventional treatments with spiritual healing in his practice, demonstrating openness to concepts beyond traditional medical approaches.
>
> He adds, "One day, I got a call from a boy I had treated. He complained about his mother's erratic behaviour and unusual demands for alcohol. Assuming it was a psychological issue, I did everything medically possible, but to no avail. That is when I recommended a visit to the sthan. When I spoke to Guruji about her, he revealed multiple spirits haunted her. In due course, she recovered with the sthan's grace."
>
> He also shares the story of a middle-aged man he had treated for a cardiac problem. Years after the initial treatment, the man started showing some bizarre symptoms. Dr Vacchani recommended a specialised heart test called an angiogram. But during the test, the man suddenly became unexplainably aggressive. After talking to him, Dr Vacchani felt as though someone—or something—else was communicating through the man. Says the good doctor, "This entity said it wanted to harm the patient but was deterred by my presence. I believe it was Gurudev's grace protecting this man and me."
> — Hingori and Dr Mahesh Vacchani, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.138–139
