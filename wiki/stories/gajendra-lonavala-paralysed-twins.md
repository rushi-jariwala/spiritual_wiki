# Gajendra Kadam Ji — The Paralysed Twins of Neral

## Full Narrative

> Gajendra Kadam ji, a sevadaar deeply committed to the seva of healing, has been a familiar presence at the Lonavala sthan since his youth. He cherishes a remarkable incident from 1997: "When Guruji (Gurudev's disciple) established the Lonavala sthan, he declared that the first 108 patients who sought treatment would receive complete healing. I can still vividly remember an incident from the early days when a tribal woman from Neral, completely paralysed, was brought in on a stretcher, carried by four men. Guruji blessed her, and to our astonishment, she not only raised herself from the stretcher but also walked back to the van in which she had arrived. The healing was instantaneous."
>
> Nevertheless, a week later, during seva, Gajendra ji found himself shocked as he witnessed the same woman, once again on a stretcher, being transported to the sthan. He disclosed, "Upon inquiry, we uncovered that she was the twin of the woman who had undergone the miraculous healing just a week earlier. Both sisters suffered from the same affliction. Just like her sister, she, too, experienced a remarkable recovery. These occurrences are indelibly etched in my memory, serving as irrefutable evidence of Gurudev's enduring healing powers."
> — Hingori and Gajendra Kadam ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.134–135
