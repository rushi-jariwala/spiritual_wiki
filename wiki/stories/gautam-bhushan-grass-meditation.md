# Gautam Bhushan ji — Becoming Grass, Leaf, and Stone

## Full Narrative

> Gautam Bhushan ji shares a remarkable experience that illustrates this universal connection: "During meditation, I experienced an incredible expansion of my being as if dispersing across vast spaces. I first became a blade of grass, deeply sensing its simple existence, marked by limited cognition but abundant feeling. I then morphed into a leaf of a lotus-like plant, a tree trunk, and a smooth stone with a distinct crack. In each state, I felt an intimate connection with these elements."
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, p.69

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, "Seva," p.69

## Related Concepts / Entities

- [[concepts/aatma]] — the universal consciousness experienced as a continuous field moving from one form to another; Aham Brahmasmi lived as meditation
- [[concepts/jivaatma]] — the individual jivaatma dissolving its sense of separate identity and recognising itself in grass, leaf, trunk, stone; the journey toward param-aatma
- [[practices/seva]] — the foundation for genuine seva is this recognition: the one served and the one serving are the same consciousness; Gautam Bhushan ji's experience makes this non-theoretical
- [[entities/mahaguru]] — who perceived every being as an extension of himself; this meditation is the experiential correlate of his lived stance

> [!tip] The chapter frames this as the experiential basis of seva: *"Only by seeing our existence reflected in the existence of others, do we truly grasp the meaning of seva."* The meditation is not an end in itself — it is a preparation for selfless service without hierarchy.
