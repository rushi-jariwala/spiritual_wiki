# Giri Ji — "Haath Ki Safaai" and the Blessed Cloves

## Full Narrative

> Giri ji's journey from scepticism to deep faith is a compelling story of healing and grace.
>
> He recounts his initial encounter with Gurudev alongside fellow Mumbaikars Shyam Dhumutkar ji and Shrikrishna Deolekar ji: "Upon meeting Gurudev for the first time, I refrained from the traditional gestures of respect, such as touching his feet. Nevertheless, he greeted me with warmth. He extended his hand to show me the natural markings on his palm. Symbols such as OM, the trishul, Ganpati, and Shivling adorned it". While this sight visibly moved Shyam ji and Shrikrishna ji, Giri ji remained unimpressed, brushing it off as mere "haath ki safaai."¹
>
> His scepticism, however, was set to undergo a dramatic change during a visit to Gurgaon for Mahashivratri in March 1983. He details that moment vividly: "Our group arrived at the sthan at 9.30 pm, but it was at 3 am that we had our turn to meet Gurudev. To my disbelief, he immediately brought up my comment about his 'haath ki safaai' from our first meeting. Initially, I suspected that my friends had tipped him off. But the following day, Gurudev clarified my unspoken thought by mentioning that he had received no information from anyone."
>
> During this visit, Gurudev showcased his extraordinary insight by mentioning Giri ji's health problems and advising against his current treatment regimen. "Instead, he handed me a few blessed cloves and cardamoms, assuring me of recovery within six months. This time, I decided to trust him and started feeling better once I returned home to Mumbai."
>
> The shift from scepticism to belief had a tangible impact on Giri ji's life. Heeding Gurudev's guidance, he sought a medical evaluation six months later. The X-ray reflected his flawless health. When he showed his previous medical records for comparison, the doctor exclaimed, "These records cannot possibly be yours!"
>
> ¹ Sleight of hand
> — Hingori and Giri ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.121–122
