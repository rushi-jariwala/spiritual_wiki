# Giri Ji's Sister — Fungal Infection and Ghee on Rotis

## Full Narrative

> When Giri ji's sister's hands became infected with fungal growth, Gurudev asked her to apply ghee on the rotis made at the sthan for eleven days. By the twelfth day, the fungal infection had subsided, leaving no trace of its stinky pus.
> — *The Guru of Gurus*, p.228

## Source

*The Guru of Gurus*, Healing — Modes of Healing, p.228.

## Notes

The prescription — applying ghee to rotis at the sthan — is counterintuitive for a fungal infection. Medically, ghee would not be an antifungal agent. The cure is attributed to the energised environment of the sthan (a spiritually charged space maintained through regular seva and mantra practice) and to Gurudev's intent as the operative agent. See [[concepts/intent-as-healing]].

## *Witnessing Greatness* Ch.9 — Giri Ji's Own Telling (p.129)

> Giri ji shares, "Fate dealt my sister a cruel hand. A severe skin condition caused her hands and feet to swell with pus and shed skin. The stench became so unbearable that no one could enter her room. Simple tasks like eating, walking, and bathing became impossible for her."
>
> He contacted Gurudev to detail his sister's plight. "Bring her to Gurgaon," the mahaguru instructed. "At Gurgaon, Gurudev's remedy was simple," Giri ji explains. "He asked her to do the langar¹ seva for 11 days, advising her to apply ghee on the rotis she helped prepare. Remarkably, by the end of the 11-day seva, the debilitating disease vanished as mysteriously as it had appeared!"
>
> ¹ A communal meal that is open to all visitors, regardless of religion, caste, gender, economic status, or ethnicity. The practice embodies the principle of seva. Meals are typically vegetarian to accommodate the dietary restrictions of all participants
> — Hingori and Giri ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, p.129

Ch.9 frames the cure as **langar seva** — an example, with [[stories/punchoo-migraine-hundred-people]], of "empowerment and the importance of paying it forward."

> [!warning] Two tellings
> *The Guru of Gurus*: "fungal growth" on her **hands**; subsided "by the twelfth day." Ch.9: a "severe skin condition" of **hands and feet** with pus and shedding skin; gone "by the end of the 11-day seva"; the ghee-on-rotis prescription is explicitly langar seva.
