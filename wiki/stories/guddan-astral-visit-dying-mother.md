# "You Did Visit Her. I Sent You" — Guddan Ji's Astral Visit to Her Dying Mother

## Full Narrative

> Guddan ji shares the story of her mother's final days: "I was living with Gurudev and Mataji in Gurgaon for five months, away from my family in Kanpur where my mother was on her death bed. Gurudev had instructed me to live separately, the reasons for which he never disclosed, and I never dared to ask. He also advised me not to inquire about my mother's condition.
>
> "Just a few days before her passing, I had an experience that is hard to define. Lying down, I saw myself flying to Kanpur to visit her. I reached her but did not touch her, conscious of the need to return to Gurgaon within the time set by Gurudev. It felt so real. After my mother's demise, I lamented that I had not met her before she died. Gurudev surprised me by saying, 'Who told you that you did not meet your mother? You did visit her. I sent you'. He reminded me of the out-of-the-body voyage to meet her before her death."
>
> Guddan ji's brother further substantiated this revelation. He recounts that their mother had sensed Guddan ji's presence and called her name, attesting to the credibility of her experience.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, pp.175–176
