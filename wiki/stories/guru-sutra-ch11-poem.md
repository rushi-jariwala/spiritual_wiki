# "He Weeds Out Ignorance" — Closing Verse of *Guru Sutra* Ch.11

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.11, p.114 (closing verse)

## Verbatim Text

> He weeds out ignorance.
> He waters the wilting soul.
> His grace is why the spirit knows
> it isn't a fractured shard
> but a complete whole.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.11, p.114

## Related Concepts and Entities

- [[concepts/customised-guidance]] — the verse captures the guru's four-segment training in five lines: removing ignorance (avidya), sustaining the discouraged disciple, transmitting grace, and establishing the jivaatma's wholeness
- [[concepts/guru-disciple]] — the guru as active weeder and waterer of the disciple's soul
- [[concepts/jivaatma]] — "a complete whole" — the jivaatma is not a fractured shard but a full expression of the consciousness supreme
- [[entities/mahaguru]] — the grace referred to is the mahaguru's kripa operating beyond teaching and instruction
