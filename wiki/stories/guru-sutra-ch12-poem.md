# Guru Sutra Ch.12 — Closing Verse

## Verbatim Text

> He fills the disciple with wisdom
>      and sets his soul aglow
>  so that one day the disciple can
> re-ignite the flame in many more.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.12, p.126

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.12, "Guru Kripa, Dakshina and Awelna," p.126. Closing verse of the chapter.

## Notes

The verse distils the entire logic of guru kripa and guru dakshina into four lines. The guru fills and ignites; the disciple becomes the carrier of that flame and re-ignites it in others. This is the chain: kripa received → wisdom and soul-glow as its form → dakshina repaid through re-igniting others.

The word "re-ignite" is important — the flame pre-exists in those who will receive it; the disciple does not create it but restores it. This is consistent with the [[concepts/guru-tattva]] teaching: the latent guru element resides in every being and is awaited by the right touch.
