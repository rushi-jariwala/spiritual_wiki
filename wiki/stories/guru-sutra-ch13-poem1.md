# "It's not a gilded throne that seats the vain"

**Source:** Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.136

> *It's not a gilded throne that seats the vain.*
> *It's not for those consumed with seeking fame.*
> *It belongs to the energy that selflessly serves*
> *man, animal and spirit in the revered guru's name.*

**Context:** This verse appears in the chapter on Transmission of Power, immediately after the description of sattvic mantra siddhis and the trishul — contextualising the *gaddi* (the guru's seat) as a place of service, not status. The gilded throne is contrasted with the seat of selfless seva.
