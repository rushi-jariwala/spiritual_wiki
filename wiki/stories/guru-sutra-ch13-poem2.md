# "Envy bludgeons reason with the sight of another on the rise"

**Source:** Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, p.140

> *Envy bludgeons reason*
> *with the sight of another on the rise.*
> *Resentment thrives, swords are drawn*
> *so he doesn't reach the prize.*

**Context:** This verse appears in the chapter's section on Spiritual Envy and Attacks, immediately after the account of the death yantra sent by post to Gurudev. It encapsulates the psychology of the spiritually envious — the attainment of another blinds reason, and resentment escalates to attempted harm.
