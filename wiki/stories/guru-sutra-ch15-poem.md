# "You can't pace the journey" — Patience Verse (*Guru Sutra* Ch.15)

Verbatim verse appearing mid-chapter, after the section on Patience. It distils Gurudev's — and Kabir's — teaching on accepting the spiritual journey's own timetable.

> You can't pace the journey.
> You can't hasten the climb.
> You have to accept that all that is to be
> will happen in its own time.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.15, p.164
