# "The stars issued an astral edict" — Poem from *Guru Sutra* Ch.16

Verbatim verse from *Guru Sutra*, Chapter 16 (*Guru by Destiny*), p.178.

> The stars issued an astral edict
>    that I would one day find
> a guru whose grace would help me
>    leave the darkness behind.

The verse is placed mid-chapter, at the point where Hingori is arguing that planetary alignments predetermine not just the disciple's spiritual inclination but the very encounter with the guru. Both guru and disciple are "born to a plan, with a coinciding destiny to support the strategy of this life."
