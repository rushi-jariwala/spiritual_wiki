# Exit the Land of Delusion — Poem (*Guru Sutra* Ch.8)

**Source:** Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.8, p.70
**Related concepts:** [[concepts/faith]] · [[concepts/maya]]

---

> Exit the land of delusion
> where falsehood lives like a king.
> Let the bridge of faith
> take you places where
> your caged soul can take wing.
>
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.8, p.70

---

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.8, p.70. Appears as an embedded verse in the chapter on faith, positioned between the Golden Principle on blind faith and the narrative section on how faith enabled Hingori to receive inner knowledge he did not consciously believe he possessed.

---

## Related Concepts

- [[concepts/faith]] — The bridge of faith is the chapter's central metaphor; this verse gives it its poetic form
- [[concepts/maya]] — "The land of delusion where falsehood lives like a king" is maya's domain
- [[concepts/guru-disciple]] — The "caged soul" is released through the guru-disciple relationship, which faith makes possible
