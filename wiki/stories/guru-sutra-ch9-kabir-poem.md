# Kabir on Surrender and the True Master — Verse

> It is for the disciple to offer himself
> Body and soul as a sacrifice at the Guru's feet;
> But the true Master will not touch
> A single penny that is offered to him.
> For my own self,
> I would rather die than beg,
> But I am not ashamed to accept offerings
> For the welfare of others.
> — Kabir, quoted in *Guru Sutra*, Ch.9, pp.83–84

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.9, "Surrender of Dhann," pp.83–84 — cited by Hingori after distinguishing genuine surrender of wealth from exploitation by false gurus.

## Related Concepts / Entities

- [[concepts/surrender]] — The verse precisely names the asymmetry: the disciple's offering is total; the true guru's acceptance is conditional — only for the welfare of others
- [[concepts/guru-disciple]] — Calibrates what the "true Master" looks like on the question of wealth: he refuses personal enrichment, accepts only what serves others
- [[concepts/non-doership]] — "I would rather die than beg / But I am not ashamed to accept offerings / For the welfare of others" — the guru's non-doership extends even to receiving: not for himself, only as instrument
