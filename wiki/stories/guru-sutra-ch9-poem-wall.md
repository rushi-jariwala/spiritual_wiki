# "Centuries of writings line the inner wall…" — Verse on Mind Cleansing

> Centuries of writings line the inner wall.
> Records of impressions and memories all.
> Purge them and cleanse them as he might
> to write new chapters to aid the spiritual flight.
> — *Guru Sutra*, Ch.9, p.86

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.9, p.86 — appears within the "Surrender of the Spiritual Self" section, following the account of Gurudev using disciples' spirits during healing.

## Related Concepts / Entities

- [[concepts/surrender]] — The "inner wall" is the karmashaya of accumulated conditioning; surrender of mann means allowing the guru to purge and rewrite it
- [[concepts/samskars]] — "Centuries of writings" are the samskars — impressions and memories carried from past lives, lining the causal body
- [[concepts/citt-vritti-nirodh]] — The purging is structurally aligned with Patanjali's nirodh: clearing the citta of accumulated vrittis to allow spiritual ascent
- [[concepts/maya]] — The accumulated conditioning is what sustains the veil of maya; cleansing it is the path to non-duality
