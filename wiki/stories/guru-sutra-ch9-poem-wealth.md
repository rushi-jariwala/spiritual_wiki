# "Owning things doesn't make you rich…" — Verse on Wealth and Seva

> Owning things doesn't make you rich
> nor the heaps of coins you've put away.
> All that you've amassed will turn to wealth
> when it is spent on enriching another's day.
> — *Guru Sutra*, Ch.9, p.80

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.9, p.80 — appears in the context of the Surrender of Tann section, facing a page depicting the sthan's seva network.

## Related Concepts / Entities

- [[concepts/surrender]] — Surrender of dhann begins with the recognition that accumulated wealth serves its real purpose only in giving
- [[practices/seva]] — The verse is a concise expression of seva's karmic logic: spending on others converts accumulation into real wealth
- [[concepts/positive-karma]] — Aligns with the Karma Sutra teaching that money spent on oneself is a debt; money spent charitably is seva
