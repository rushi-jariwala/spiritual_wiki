# A Symbolic Offering of the Ego — Poem

Verbatim verse appearing between the Modesty and Guru's Popularity sections of *Guru Sutra*, Ch.14, pp.154.

> A symbolic offering of the ego
> so his grace can be the guide
> that turns the shackled soul into a giant
> free of delusion, ignorance and pride.

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.14, p.154. Attributed to Hingori (no explicit attribution given; consistent with authorial verse style throughout the book).

## Notes

The verse encapsulates the core logic of guru vandana: the offering is not of material goods or ritual actions but of the ego itself. What is given up is delusion, ignorance, and pride — what is received is the grace that frees. The "shackled soul" is the jivaatma bound by maya; the guru's grace is the mechanism of liberation. The poem bridges the two halves of the chapter: the personal practices of revering the guru (touching feet, meditation) and the guru's own relationship with humility and fame.
