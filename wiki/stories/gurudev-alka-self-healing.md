# Alka ji Heals Her Arm — Learn to Heal Yourself

## Full Narrative

> "He used very few words to explain things," Alka ji notes, "but those words had a deeper meaning. I used to get sick quite often as a kid. When I went to Daddy, he would bless cardamoms and cloves and give them to me. I also took medicines prescribed by the local doctor. 'Learn to heal yourself,' he remarked one day. I had no idea what he meant. Years later, after Daddy had moved on, I was experiencing severe pain in my arm, and his words began to ring in my ears. I focused on my arm for a minute or two while chanting my mantras. Though the pain subsided, I assumed it was temporary relief and that the pain would return. However, when the pain did not return for another 15 days, I was confident that I was completely healed. I realised what Daddy meant—if we have self-belief, we can heal ourselves."

— Alka ji (Gurudev's daughter), *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, p.83

- [[concepts/guru-kripa]] — the teaching arrives after his physical passing; guru kripa is not bound to the guru's presence
