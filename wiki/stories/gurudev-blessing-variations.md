# Gurudev Blessing Variations

## The Story

Narrated in *Guru Sutra*, Ch.14, pp.147–148:

> I have watched Gurudev blessing thousands of people, one after another, for hours on end, without respite. Due to a well-developed power of observation, I noticed every blessing was not the same, since both, his facial expressions as well as his body language differed, depending on whom he was blessing. Sometimes, he would bless with both hands (I believe this was the most powerful blessing he would give), sometimes he would bless people with his left hand, while at other times, with his right, sometimes he would show a hint of emotion, and at other times, he was emotionless and expressionless.
>
> I have been blessed by him several hundred times and have felt the strength of the blessings. A very memorable case was one where I saw a vision of getting hurt and bleeding in an accident. When I sought his permission, soon thereafter to go on a journey, he grudgingly gave me approval on my insistence. I remember, he blessed me with both hands quite dramatically. The accident did happen but without the bleeding that was meant to happen as foreseen in the vision.
>
> Was my vision defective or his blessing effective?

## Source

*Guru Sutra*, Ch.14, "Touching the Guru's Feet", pp.147–148.

## Notes

The scooter accident referenced is narrated in full in [[stories/scooter-accident-vision]] (*Karma Sutra*, Ch.1, p.23). The Ch.14 version adds the detail that Gurudev blessed with both hands "quite dramatically" — and contextualises this as the most powerful form of blessing, given in response to a foreseen danger. Hingori's question — "Was my vision defective or his blessing effective?" — recurs across two books as a deliberate rhetorical pivot.
