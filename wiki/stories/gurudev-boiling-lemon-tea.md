# Gurudev's Hands in Boiling Lemon Tea — Rashmi Ji Sees It Thrice

## Full Narrative

> During every Mahashivratri at Gurgaon, a remarkable event would unfold, mystifying those who witnessed it. As part of a ritual, Gurudev would bless the boiling lemon tea in a manner that seemed to defy the laws of nature. He would immerse his hands into the scalding liquid for a few seconds, yet surprisingly, his hands remained unscathed.
>
> Gurudev's sister Rashmi ji recounts her initial scepticism and eventual astonishment at what she witnessed: "I had a hard time believing that Gurudev could place his hands in boiling tea without harming himself."
>
> Her disbelief was challenged when she witnessed this extraordinary act in person. She marvels, "I saw with my own eyes how Gurudev fearlessly placed his hands into boiling tea, not just once but thrice. Each instance left me more amazed than the last. In fact, on the third occasion, he submerged his hands even deeper into the vessel. Yet, remarkably, his hands remained burn free."
>
> This astonishing feat was more than a demonstration of the mahaguru's physical endurance. It spoke volumes about his alignment with all life forms, elements, and cosmic energies.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, p.168

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony), p.168 (illustration: "Gurudev submerges his hands in boiling lemon tea," p.169).

## Notes

- *The Guru of Gurus* (p.243) credits the feat to the Mahagayatri: "The Mahagayatri mantra confers containment of fire" — "without a flinch, wince, or blister," on "most" Mahashivratri nights. Ch.12 says "every Mahashivratri" and reads it as "his alignment with all life forms, elements, and cosmic energies." See [[synthesis/mantra-and-water]].
