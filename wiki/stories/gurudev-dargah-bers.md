# Plucking Bers at the Dargah

## Full Narrative

> "Our shared adventures often led us to nearby fields, where we would pick fruits or get up to some other mischief, only to face the consequences when we got home," Subbhash ji chuckles. "On our way home from school was a dargah. We would visit it to pluck bers from the trees that grew nearby. The fakir who lived there would say, 'You create a lot of trouble; at least bow your head here'. I found out much later that Gurudev often visited the dargah alone."

— Subbhash Sabbharwal ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, p.11
