# Shared Apartment in East Delhi

## Full Narrative

> Nagpal ji resided in a modest apartment in East Delhi, sharing the space with his landlord and longtime friend, Dwarkanath ji, who fondly recalls, "Nagpal and I went back to our days as youngsters in Agra. After relocating to Delhi, we secured jobs and became roommates. One day, Nagpal mentioned that a colleague from Hariana was searching for a place to stay. I was open to the idea, suggesting he could move in with us if we got along well. Upon meeting Gurudev, I was immediately struck by his humility and intellect. Consequently, the three of us agreed to share the apartment."
>
> "Gurudev's job as a soil surveyor required him to travel extensively, so he was only home for about half the year. I yearned for his delightful company when he was away on tours. It is worth noting that my elder brother only came to visit when Gurudev was around," Dwarkanath ji chuckles. "Gurudev had a way of telling jokes and sharing anecdotes that left everyone wanting more. Such was his charisma that every meeting transformed casual acquaintances into ardent admirers."

— Dwarkanath ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, p.16
