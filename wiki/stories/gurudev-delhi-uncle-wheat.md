# Relocation to Delhi and Sacks of Wheat

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev's strong inclination towards spirituality over academics persuaded his family that moving to Delhi might steer him towards a more conventional and successful career path. After completing his tenth grade, Gurudev relocated to Delhi, where he lived under the care of his uncle in Shahdara.
>
> Life in India's bustling capital was not without its trials for young Gurudev. Ever the pragmatist, he embraced any available job to support himself. At his uncle's home, he was not merely a guest but an integral part of the household, contributing to daily chores. Frequently, he would carry heavy sacks of wheat to the grinding mill, epitomising the spirit of dedication as he sought to repay his gratitude towards his uncle and his family.
>
> Throughout his life, the mahaguru embodied and championed a lifestyle free from debt. He exemplified the virtues of generosity over personal gain, setting a powerful example for others.

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, p.15
