# The Flight Money That Materialised — Hingori's Monthly Bada Guruvar

## Full Narrative

> Occasionally, Gurudev would reach into his pockets and, to everyone's surprise, pull out a bundle of notes. I wonder if he begrudgingly wielded some power to conjure up the currency, using it solely in circumstances that demanded nisvarth seva. It seems plausible that this extraordinary ability was granted to him later in life as an acknowledgement of his steadfast altruism and complete detachment from money.
>
> I have seen this gift of his manifest in my life. At Gurudev's insistence, I travelled to Gurgaon monthly for Bada Guruvar. The mahaguru insisted that I fly instead of taking the train. Miraculously, during those years of frequent financial shortfalls, the necessary funds for the flight tickets would somehow materialise—found under my mattress, hidden in a cupboard drawer, or secreted in a coat pocket. I am confident that the mahaguru's benevolence was at work.

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, pp.17–18

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1 "Back To The Future," pp.17–18.

## What It Illustrates

This is a personal account from Hingori — distinct from the more programmatic story of [[stories/hingori-dhann-airfare]] (*Guru Sutra* Ch.9), where Gurudev insists Hingori fly and frames the compliance as "an earning for eternity." The *Witnessing Greatness* account adds the operative *mechanism*: the money to comply with the instruction appeared from the surface of Hingori's own daily life — under the mattress, in a drawer, in a coat pocket. The instruction created the conditions for itself to be obeyed.

The chapter frames this within a broader pattern: Gurudev's own "bundles of notes" surfacing only for *nisvarth seva*, and never for personal use. The same power — currency materialising as needed — was extended to his disciples when they were carrying out his instructions.

> [!tip] The mechanism inverts the usual relationship between resource and intent. Ordinarily, money permits action. Here, action *summons* money — but only when the action is directed by the guru's will and serves seva. See [[concepts/surrender]] on the same paradox in the dhann dimension.
