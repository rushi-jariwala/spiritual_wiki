# Gurudev's Illness and Recovery at Baba Balak Nath

## Full Narrative

> In 1948, Gurudev was struck by a debilitating illness after consuming food offered to him by an acquaintance. Despite receiving medical attention, he found no relief and was eventually bed-ridden. Desperate for a cure, his mother insisted on taking him to the famous temple of Baba Balak Nath in Himachal Pradesh. His parents undertook the herculean task of carrying their immobile son across many kilometres of mountain road leading to the temple. On reaching there, a disciple of Baba Balak Nath gave Gurudev jal to drink, and on drinking it, he vomited buttermilk and masaan (ashes).

— Hingori, *The Guru of Gurus*, p.8–9

> Gurudev's wife, Mataji, believed the miraculous recovery that followed left an indelible mark on him and may have triggered his quest for spiritual knowledge.

— Hingori, *The Guru of Gurus*, p.10

> Gurudev's wife, reverentially known as Mataji, shares an insightful episode from her husband's childhood: "When he was in the fifth or sixth grade, an acquaintance gave him something infused with negative energy to eat. This made him quite ill, and he was bedridden for about two years. When conventional medical treatments failed to heal him, my mother-in-law proposed seeking help from the Baba Balak Nath temple in Hamirpur, which is about 103 kilometres from Hariana. Despite my father-in-law's reservations, my mother-in-law's conviction won out, and together, they embarked on the challenging journey to the temple, hopeful for a cure. At the temple, a devotee of Baba Balak Nath gave Gurudev jal to drink, leading him to vomit a mixture of undigested buttermilk and ashes from a masaan. Only after this purge did his health begin to improve."

— Mataji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, pp.12–13
