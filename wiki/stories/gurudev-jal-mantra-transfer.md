# Jal as Medium of Spiritual Transmission

## Full Narrative

### Sipped Water: Sharing Spiritual Energy

> Gurudev used water in unique ways. He would sometimes sip from a glass of water and then pass the rest to someone else. This act was a way of sharing his spiritual energy and qualities with the other person, sometimes to cure and sometimes to help them on their spiritual journey. For example, he often gave his disciples mantras by sharing his sipped water with them, saying, "I've transferred 10,000 recitations of this mantra to you". Such statements signified that he was imparting some of his extensive spiritual practices, such as mantra chanting, to bolster their spiritual development.

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, pp.97–98

### Guru Purnima: Washing the Feet, Drinking the Water

> On special days such as Guru Purnima, a tradition involved disciples washing Gurudev's feet with water. The disciples would then drink this water infused with his spiritual energy. This was a way to receive the mahaguru's blessings, deepening their bond with him.

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.98

## Source

## The Broader Water Teaching

The chapter situates both practices within a wider understanding of water as a spiritually responsive medium:

> Water, often described as the planet's lifeblood, reflects the emotions and intentions of those interacting with it. Despite retaining its chemical composition, water's molecular structure can change, manifesting its dual potential to heal and harm.

— *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, pp.85–87

This dual potential is demonstrated across the chapter in both positive and negative instances. At Gurudev's sthans, jal is used for healing — infused with powerful mantras, it becomes a medium of his spiritual energy. The Kamlesh incident stands at the negative pole: a young girl from Gurgaon unknowingly drank sherbet mixed with a harmful taweez, illustrating water's equal capacity to carry destructive intent. See [[stories/kamlesh-black-magic-cure]].

> [!tip] Water's molecular structure changes without changing its chemical composition. This is the mechanism underlying both Gurudev's sipped-water mantra transfer and the contaminated sherbet — the same medium, opposite intentions. See [[concepts/intent-as-healing]] for how the mahaguru's intent is always the operative force.
