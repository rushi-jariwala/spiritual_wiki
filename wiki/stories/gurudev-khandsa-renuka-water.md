# Gurudev Finds Water — Khandsa Farm and the Renuka Canal

## Full Narrative

> In the late 1980s, we encountered a significant hurdle at the Khandsa farm: the only available water was saline and unsuitable for agriculture. Recognising the critical need for a viable water source, Gurudev confidently guided us to a particular spot on the farm and instructed us to dig. To our astonishment, we unearthed an underground reservoir of water directly beneath that spot. As we dug further, freshwater surfaced, promising sustainable irrigation for the farm's future.
>
> Years earlier, Gurudev had faced a similar predicament in an arid region of Renuka, where he and his team established a soil survey camp. Alongside Bittu ji and a labourer, he ascended a nearby mountain. On their descent, Gurudev instructed Bittu ji to trace the path he walked with a stick, outlining where a canal could be dug. He directed the labourer to follow this line with his digging. Miraculously, as the labourer dug, water began to surge forth, forming a canal that flowed directly to their campsite. This newly sourced water not only sustained them during their stay but also revitalised the dry terrain into a lush green sanctuary. However, once they vacated the camp, the water receded to its source, and the land reverted to its barren state.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, p.163

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony), p.163. The chapter's opening episode.

## Notes

- *The Guru of Gurus* (Supernature, pp.241–243) records the same two episodes briefly: potable water found on the Khandsa farm "when an earlier borewell had failed," and arid land at Renuka turned green by a water channel. Ch.12 adds Bittu ji tracing the line with a stick, the labourer digging along it — and that the water **receded** once the camp was vacated. See the tension flagged in [[sources/witnessing-greatness]] (Ch.12).
- The chapter follows at once with: "Water was central to Gurudev's spiritual practices, serving as a key medium for sharing his spiritual energy" (p.163). See [[synthesis/mantra-and-water]].
