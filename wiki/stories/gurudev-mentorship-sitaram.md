# Mentorship by the Sitaram Gurus

## Full Narrative

> RC Malhotra ji, the mahaguru's first disciple, shares, "Sitaram ji of Dasua began living at the Sheetla Mata temple near Gurudev's house. His guru, Sitaram ji of Benaras, who lived in Haridwar, later joined him. They both mentored Gurudev."
>
> Together, they provided young Gurudev with a structured approach to spirituality. They guided him in acquiring quite a few siddhis, which allowed him to heal diseases and grant wishes. While these early abilities were instrumental in teaching him mental discipline, focus, perseverance, and resilience, they were just the beginning of his spiritual journey. Years later, under the guidance of his guru, Buddhe Baba, Gurudev transcended these initial siddhis, embarking on a spiritual path that led to his eventual emergence as a mahaguru.

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, p.14
