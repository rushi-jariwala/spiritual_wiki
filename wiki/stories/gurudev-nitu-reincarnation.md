# Nitu ji — Reincarnation of Gurudev's Father

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev frequently stated that his five children embodied the reincarnated spirits of his ancestors and spiritual connections. He ensured they received a proper upbringing and education, faithfully fulfilling his familial responsibilities and discharging his karmic debts.
>
> Many are convinced that his youngest son, Nitu ji, is the reincarnation of Gurudev's father. Gurudev had confided in Mataji that his father would return as their son to participate in the wedding of Gurudev's younger brother, whom we affectionately called Chacha ji. Astonishingly, Nitu ji was born just one week before the scheduled wedding festivities. Gurudev addressed Nitu ji as 'Pandit ji', the same way his father had been addressed in the past.
>
> Renu ji, believed to have a spiritual connection from the past, reflects, "In my childhood, I hardly grasped the essence of spiritualism. Daddy was like a magnet, drawing large crowds. I knew he had this unique ability to heal and read minds. My siblings and I followed his practice of chanting mantras. I remember we used to chant the Gayatri mantra before sleep. Daddy never imposed strict rituals on us; there was no insistence on offering prasad at the sthan or performing aarti. Everything we embraced in our spiritual practices later in life was out of choice, not obligation."

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, p.78
