# A Shahdara Walk and the PUSA Career

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev's early life was woven from a series of events that seemed to stitch together the pieces of his destiny. During a walk in Shahdara, he aided a stranger who turned out to be the spouse of the principal at Delhi's PUSA Institute. This meeting led to Gurudev's enrollment in a technical course at the Bharat Sevak Samaj, setting him on a path to becoming a soil surveyor for the Government of India. This job seemed designed to extend his future spiritual influence into India's remote areas, reaching far beyond urban centres.

— Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, p.15

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Chapter 1 ("Back To The Future"), p.15
