# Gurudev Sleeps on the Floor for His "Brother" Ravan

## Full Narrative

> Ravan, the antagonist from Ramayana, has a rich and complex legacy. While some celebrate him as a learned scholar, a mighty king, and a devoted disciple of Shiv, he is also remembered as the embodiment of arrogance and destruction. Despite these contradictions, I affectionately refer to him as Chacha ji, a title inspired by a story shared by Rajpal ji.
>
> Rajpal ji recalls an incident around Dussehra when Ravan's effigies are typically burned: "The night before Dussehra, Gurudev announced his wish to sleep on the floor instead of his bed. Surprised, I asked him the reason. Gurudev explained that it was the day his brother had passed away, and he wanted to show respect by sleeping on the floor."
>
> Digging deeper, Rajpal ji learnt from Gurudev that this 'brother' was, in fact, Ravan. Shedding light on their relationship, Gurudev stated, "Both Ravan and I are disciples of Shiv, which spiritually binds us as gurubhais."
>
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.8, pp.105–106
