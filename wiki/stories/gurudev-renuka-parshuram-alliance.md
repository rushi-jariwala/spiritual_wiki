# Gurudev and Parshuram Ji Form a Spiritual Alliance at Renuka

## Full Narrative

> Renuka, a small town in the Sirmaur District of Himachal Pradesh, is believed to be part of the spiritual domain of Parshuram ji, the immortal son of Renuka Devi and her husband, Saptarishi Jamadagni.
>
> In 1980, Gurudev set up camp in Renuka on a plot owned by a local politician, Shri Chandramani Vashisht. After settling in, Gurudev asked Bittu ji to clean and refurbish an old shivling situated on the campsite.
>
> Vashisht ji posed the question to the mahaguru when he returned a few days later: he had been seeing images of various deities flash sequentially in meditation, always ending with the image of a bespectacled man in pants and a shirt. Gurudev showed him the OM on his hand after serving him a cup of tea. On seeing the luminous symbol, he prostrated at Gurudev's feet. "Son, I was supposed to come here in 1970, but I arrived in 1980," Gurudev said with a smile. "You've been waiting for me for ten years!"
>
> Gurudev is said to have had spiritual meetings with Parshuram ji before beginning seva at Renuka. In 1980, they formed an alliance that resulted in the establishment of a sthan. Vashisht ji, a disciple of Gurudev and Parshuram ji, took over the reins. Before his death, he appointed his disciple, Dinesh ji, to carry the baton of seva. It is believed that Parshuram ji still visits the sthan to keep an eye on things.
>
> In addition to Parshuram ji and Renuka Devi, Gurudev set up camps in many places and formed alliances with the deities who ruled there. These include Khawaja Moinuddin Chishti, Shri Markandeshwar, Baba Balak Nath, Ganpati, the Sikh Gurus (Guru Nanak Dev, Guru Gobind Singh, and Guru Angad Dev), Hanuman ji, the Ashvin Kumaras, Dattatreya, Sai Baba of Shirdi, Indra, and possibly many others.
> — Hingori, *The Guru of Gurus*, pp.77–81

## *Witnessing Greatness* Version (Ch.8, pp.100–101)

> A defining moment in Gurudev's spiritual journey took place during his camp at Renuka in Himachal Pradesh, where he cultivated an enduring spiritual alliance with the legendary Parshuram ji. This momentous partnership was not just a matter of chance; it necessitated a perfect blend of tact, diplomacy, and spiritual synergy.
>
> Ravi ji explains, "Renuka is the spiritual domain of Parshuram ji, the son of Devi Renuka. Such is the sanctity of the region that no spiritual entity can venture there without Parshuram ji's consent. On Gurudev's first visit to Renuka, he saw Parshuram ji in a colossal 27-foot form. However, Parshuram ji's height changed as he recognised Gurudev's spiritual depth. In their subsequent meetings, Parshuram ji appeared in diminishing sizes, from 18 feet to his regular stature of nine feet. While Parshuram ji granted Gurudev the opportunity to reside in Renuka, Gurudev's disciples could only visit the place after the duo achieved a spiritual working relationship."
>
> The alliance that Gurudev and Parshuram ji formed in 1980 led to the establishment of a sthan at Bayri, close to Renuka, which has since become a source of solace for countless beings. It is widely held that Parshuram ji's guiding hand still graces the sthan, aiding their shared mission of nisvarth seva.
>
> Chandramani Vashisht ji, who had the privilege of becoming Gurudev's first disciple in Renuka, was deeply devoted to Parshuram ji. His contributions to the restoration and expansion of temples at Renuka were noteworthy. Naturally, he emerged as the unanimous choice to oversee the operations of the sthan.
>
> Chandramani ji's disciple and the current custodian of the Bayri sthan, Dinesh ji, and his wife are convinced that the immortal Parshuram ji often visits the sthan incognito, keenly observing its proceedings. A tale that resonates with their conviction revolves around the time of the sthan's renovation. A panther mysteriously appeared and began to guard the site each night, posing no threat to anyone. It vanished once the renovations were over, never to be seen again.
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.8, pp.100–101

New in this version: the 27 → 18 → 9-foot diminishing forms; the rule of consent over the domain; the sthan named as **Bayri**; the guardian panther.
