# Roommate Mysteries and Unexplained Conveniences

## Full Narrative

> Dwarkanath ji reflects on Gurudev's early spiritual inclinations with a sense of awe. "Living together for so long, we were oblivious of his spiritual powers. He was far more evolved than we realised. While we slept, he often rose, unfolded his cot, wrapped himself in a blanket, and meditated for hours. Nagpal would question him, and he would say he was doing his paath. He often made predictions that would invariably come true. And then there were the unexplained conveniences, like arriving at a movie theatre without tickets, only to find someone waiting with exactly three tickets for us. We experienced many such mysterious events."

— Dwarkanath ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.1, p.18
