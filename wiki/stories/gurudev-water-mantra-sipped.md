# Gurudev Shares Sipped Water — Mantra Transfer and Guru Purnima

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev used water in unique ways. He would sometimes sip from a glass of water and then pass the rest to someone else. This act was a way of sharing his spiritual energy and qualities with the other person, sometimes to cure and sometimes to help them on their spiritual journey. For example, he often gave his disciples mantras by sharing his sipped water with them, saying, "I've transferred 10,000 recitations of this mantra to you". Such statements signified that he was imparting some of his extensive spiritual practices, such as mantra chanting, to bolster their spiritual development.
>
> On special days such as Guru Purnima, a tradition involved disciples washing Gurudev's feet with water. The disciples would then drink this water infused with his spiritual energy. This was a way to receive the mahaguru's blessings, deepening their bond with him.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.93

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, "Deception of Perception," p.93

## Context

Water is established across the sources as one of the primary leitmotifs of Gurudev's life — he was born through the blessings of *jal*, healed through *jal* at Baba Balak Nath, and wielded *jal* as a healing instrument throughout his ministry. See [[sources/witnessing-greatness]] (Ch.1 themes).

The chapter contextualises this with a broader principle: despite retaining its chemical composition, water's molecular structure can change, manifesting its dual potential to heal and harm. *Jal* charged with powerful mantras at Gurudev's *sthans* was documented as a medium of healing energy. See [[quotes/healing]].

The shared-sip practice is the most intimate form of this — not *jal* energised at a distance, but water that has passed through the mahaguru's own body and carried whatever spiritual charge he had accumulated through lakhs of mantra recitations.

The declaration "I've transferred 10,000 recitations of this mantra to you" maps directly onto the [[concepts/mantra-transfer]] mechanism documented in *The Guru of Gurus* and *Guru Sutra*: paath-hours can be allocated from guru to disciple.
