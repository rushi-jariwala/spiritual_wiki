# Hari Ram — The Spirit Who Asked for Rebirth

## Full Narrative

> Throughout human history, how rare is it to encounter beings to whom spirits themselves come, pleading for help?
>
> Nikku ji, Mataji's nephew, shares a remarkable encounter: "One day, after Gurudev had returned early from work and completed his paath, we were sitting, awaiting tea. Mid-conversation, his attention was caught by a man walking past the window. He turned to me with purpose and said, 'Call that man inside. I have something to show you'. My curiosity was instantly piqued.
>
> "The man entered the sthan and bowed before Gurudev. Gurudev then gave a simple and enigmatic command, 'Okay, call him!' Following Gurudev's directions, the man carefully removed his jewellery and laid it next to Gurudev's bed. With a subtle flick of his fingers and a gentle turn of his head, a spirit within the man emerged. 'What is your name?' Gurudev inquired. The response, 'Hari Ram', left me in awe, for I realised this was not the man's name but that of the spirit."
>
> Nikku ji recounts the intense dialogue between Gurudev and Hari Ram, "Gurudev asked, 'Where did you take hold of him?' The spirit admitted, 'One evening at Nigambhod Ghat when he was urinating under a tree'. Gurudev often advised against urinating under the trees or when facing the sun, warning of the spiritual risks involved. When Gurudev commanded the spirit to leave, it hesitated, seeking Gurudev's gaze before making a request, 'I will depart from this man, but only if you promise me rebirth'. With a knowing smile, Gurudev responded, 'We will see'. At those words, the spirit relinquished its hold, leaving the man to collect his belongings, receive Gurudev's blessings, and take his leave."
> — Hingori and Nikku ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.126–127
