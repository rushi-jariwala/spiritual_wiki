# Nocturnal Attack by Neighbourhood Temple Deity

**Source:** Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, pp.141–142

> In a more recent incident, I was attacked in my sleep by what I believe was the energy of the deity of an old, ramshackled temple in my neighbourhood. The deity attacked me in the form of an outlandish, woman-like creature with a whitish face, from a different time period. This strangely dressed creature was the most bizarre feminine form I had seen. I could have easily visited the temple in my waking state and destroyed the energy equation there. But, was I willing to stoop to the level of the deity? I decided to ignore the attack. I knew why the deity was hitting out. A few years earlier, we had started a temple in the same neighbourhood to further the path of seva. I guess the deity did not like the idea of another temple gaining significance through our efforts. Thanks only to the training I had received in self-protection that I could withstand and overcome such attacks. I have trained sevadaars in the art of self-preservation.

**Context:** The attack came during sleep — a particularly vulnerable state. The attacking entity manifested as a bizarrely dressed woman-like creature with a whitish face "from a different time period" — consistent with an old, accumulated energy that had long been dormant in a ramshackle neighbourhood temple. The motive was territorial: a new temple nearby was growing in significance.

Hingori's response is notable. He acknowledges he had the power to go to the temple in his waking state and "destroy the energy equation" — but refused to stoop to that level. Instead he ignored the attack, weathered it through his training in self-preservation, and moved on.

Two contrasting stances appear across the chapter:
- [[stories/sthan-hostile-temple]]: pre-emptive diplomacy (sweetmeats) neutralises a different hostile temple.
- This story: post-attack non-retaliation, letting the attack exhaust itself.

Both are presented as correct in context. The test of self-preservation training is precisely the capacity to choose non-escalation even when escalation is possible.
