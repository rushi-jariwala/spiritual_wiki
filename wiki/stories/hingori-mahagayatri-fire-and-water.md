# Hingori — The Mahagayatri's Fire and Water

## Full Narrative

> The Mahagayatri mantra embodies a unique duality of elemental energies, as it ignites the fire element within practitioners and has a deep affinity with the water element.
>
> I recall an episode from my early dalliances with the mantra. Engulfed by the mantra's fiery force, I lost control of my temper, reacting harshly towards my housekeeper, Khemchand, who had sown seeds of discord within my family. I pushed him in thoughtless anger, watching as he stumbled backwards. That moment was a grounding reflection of my behaviour and a testament to the influencing power of the mantra vibrating within me. Regular hydration is advisable to mitigate the intense heat generated by this mantra, helping to prevent physical unease or mental agitation.
>
> The Mahagayatri mantra's connection with the element of water is of deep spiritual significance. Upon seeing a vision of a woman dressed in jeans swimming effortlessly underwater, a scene I felt carried deeper significance, I consulted with Gurudev. He interpreted this vision as an indication that my practice of the Mahagayatri mantra had reached a state of siddhi. This insight aligns with the experiences of many practitioners of this mantra, who frequently report dreams and visions involving water bodies, suggesting a deep spiritual synergy between this mantra and the water element.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11, pp.154–155

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11 (Sacred Syllables), pp.154–155.
