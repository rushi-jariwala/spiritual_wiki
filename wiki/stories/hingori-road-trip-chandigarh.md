# The Chandigarh Road Trip Argument — Gurudev Hears from Gurgaon

## Full Narrative

> This remarkable ability revealed itself during a road trip I took with my father from Chandigarh to Gurgaon. The journey was fraught with tension, as we argued most of the way. Upon arriving in Gurgaon, Gurudev gently yet promptly admonished me for my behaviour, fully aware of the entire exchange. His vigilance was such that nothing went unnoticed.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.95

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.95

## Context

The chapter frames this incident within Gurudev's evolution from ordinary listener to supernormal hearer. In his youth he enjoyed popular music (Binaca Geet Mala on radio; whistling tunes; playing the mouth organ). As he progressed spiritually, his relationship with auditory input transformed: he used hearing as a spiritual tool — perceiving spoken words and even thoughts from great distances.

The Chandigarh-to-Gurgaon distance is approximately 260 km. Gurudev, seated in Gurgaon, was fully aware of an argument that occurred several hours earlier on a moving car journey far away. His admonishment was "gentle yet prompt" — not punitive, but impossible to ignore.

The experience taught Hingori two things: that the senses can be influenced and extended far beyond their normal range, and that genuine spiritual advancement leaves no room for pretence in the presence of such a teacher.
