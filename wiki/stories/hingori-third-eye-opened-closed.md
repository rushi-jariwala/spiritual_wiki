# Gurudev Opens and Closes Hingori's Third Eye

## Source

*Aatma Sutra*, Ch.VIII (Tapa Lok section), pp.92–93; *Guru Sutra*, Ch.11, pp.111–112

## Full Narrative

### Aatma Sutra Version

> 35 years ago was when Gurudev first opened my third eye. This blessed me with several experiences which I shared with him. What seemed cruel at that time, was that he scanned his hand from my head to my lower back and shut my third eye. I could have otherwise answered any question on any subject to anyone at any time. The reason he did this was because he did not want me to get stuck at the level of the third eye, enjoying the capabilities and amazements which would not allow me to move further. Mine was not a unique case, as he did this to most of his disciples, whom he helped qualify for even higher loks. He disallowed the usage of the third eye capability, lest they become obstacles to their progress.
> — Hingori, *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.VIII, pp.92–93

### Guru Sutra Version — With the Interior Moment

> I realised my third eye was in the process of opening, since I was experiencing visions of the future and hearing an inner voice. I shared my experiences with Gurudev. Since he was not in favour of the opening of the third eye, he put his hand on my head and ran it down my spine. I sensed he was lowering my kundalini. I felt depressed. Why was he doing this to me? Answering my unspoken question, he said he did not want me to get stuck at the forehead chakra. He felt I might stagnate at the level of the third eye, enjoying my status as a psychic, telling people what the future had in store for them and describing events before they transpired. Though it could have been a great advantage and an enjoyable one, it could have also become an impediment. The temptation of showing off could have been a threat to progress of the self.
>
> I realise that in spiritual evolution, you should not get stuck at a particular level of attainment. You must transcend and go beyond it.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.11, pp.111–112
