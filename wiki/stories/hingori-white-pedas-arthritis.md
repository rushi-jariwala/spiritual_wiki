# White Pedas and Hingori's Arthritis

## Full Narrative

> Before my life-changing experience with Gurudev, I fell victim to such negative influences. Someone used white *pedas* to channel harmful energy towards me. These innocent-looking sweetmeats were charged with negativity through specific rituals and reverse mantras, contributing to my development of severe arthritis. For this reason, it is advisable to add spices such as turmeric or black pepper to white or light-coloured foods to change their colour and pre-empt the impact of any potential negative energy that may be present in one's environment.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, pp.91–92

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, "Deception of Perception," pp.91–92

## Context

The chapter frames this testimony within the principle that light-coloured foods — particularly white dishes — are especially receptive to subtle energies, both positive and negative. The same quality that makes food a vehicle for a cook's loving intention also makes it a medium for negative practices.

Hingori offers a practical countermeasure: adding spices such as turmeric or black pepper to white or light-coloured foods changes their colour and neutralises their susceptibility to negative energy absorption.

> [!tip] This account links the cause of Hingori's severe arthritis to a spiritual attack before his meeting with Gurudev. *Guru Sutra* Ch.13 identifies the perpetrator as a temple priest — see [[concepts/spiritual-attacks]]. The WG Ch.7 account specifies white *pedas* as the delivery medium but does not name the attacker.

The healing from this arthritis — in a single minute, by Gurudev — is the founding moment of Hingori's discipleship. See [[stories/hingori-arthritis-cure]].

A parallel incident involving a young girl named Kamlesh, who unknowingly drank sherbet mixed with a harmful *taweez*, is briefly referenced in the same chapter. See [[stories/kamlesh-black-magic-cure]].
