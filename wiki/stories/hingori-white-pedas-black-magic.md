# White Pedas and the Black Magic That Caused Hingori's Arthritis

## Full Narrative

> Before my life-changing experience with Gurudev, I fell victim to such negative influences. Someone used white pedas to channel harmful energy towards me. These innocent-looking sweetmeats were charged with negativity through specific rituals and reverse mantras, contributing to my development of severe arthritis.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.92

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, p.92

## Context

This anecdote is told in the context of Hingori's teaching about light-coloured foods as energy receptors. The incident occurred before his encounter with Gurudev — a period referenced elsewhere as the time when Hingori was susceptible to negative influences (see [[stories/hingori-arthritis-cure]] for the healing that followed).

The white *pedas* — a traditional Indian sweetmeat made from condensed milk, sugar, and cardamom — were selected precisely for their colour: light-coloured foods absorb ambient and intentionally directed energies more readily than dark-spiced foods. The individual who deployed them charged them through specific rituals and reverse mantras before giving them to Hingori.

The practical protection Gurudev taught in response to this principle: add spices such as turmeric or black pepper to white or light-coloured foods. This changes their colour and pre-empts the impact of potential negative energy — whether intentionally directed or merely absorbed from a hostile environment.
