# Humsa Dhir Ji Healed by a Being of Orange-Golden Light

**Source:** *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3 — Impossible Is Possible, p.34–35

## Full Narrative

> Humsa Dhir ji has been a devoted sevadaar for many decades. At the age of 35, she developed muscle fatigue that severely limited her mobility, requiring her to use a cane to walk. Over time, her condition deteriorated — both legs grew stiff and unresponsive to treatment — plunging her into the throes of negativity. Feeling overwhelmed by the well-intentioned but excessive sympathy from friends and family, Humsa ji reached a decisive moment: "I resolved to bravely face my circumstances, accepting my physical condition and whatever destiny had laid out for me."
>
> That same night, a remarkable event occurred: "I suddenly awoke to see a tall being made entirely of orange-golden light doing something to the lower half of my body."
>
> Initially filled with fear, she watched as the figure stopped its actions and dissolved into the wall behind her bed. "After the initial shock faded, I cautiously touched my leg and attempted to lift it. To my amazement, it moved lightly and rose effortlessly — a feat I had not achieved in many months." She experienced the same miraculous ease with her other leg.
>
> "I was healed," Humsa ji states confidently. "And I believe that the radiant light form was none other than Gurudev."
> — Humsa Dhir ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.34–35

## Context

The healing follows immediately upon Humsa ji's inner resolution to accept her fate without bitterness — a private act of surrender with no external audience. This timing is significant in the tradition: the mahaguru's intervention often arrives precisely when the disciple stops demanding relief and chooses acceptance. See [[concepts/destiny]] and [[concepts/non-doership]].

The "orange-golden light" form mirrors the blinding luminous forms reported in other testimonies: Kwatra ji's room of "hundreds of suns" ([[stories/kwatra-ji-blinding-light]]) and Shambhu ji's vision of Gurudev's body "bathed in dazzling golden light" ([[stories/shambhuji-five-headed-form]]). The figure dissolving into the wall aligns with the seh sharir capacity of evolved saints — dematerialisation — described in [[concepts/sukshma-sharir]].
