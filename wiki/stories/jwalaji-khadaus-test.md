# The Khadaus of Jwalaji — Gurudev Tested by the Temple Priests

## Full Narrative

> In Himachal, aptly named devobhoomi, the resonance of spirituality and the acceptance of miracles are as familiar as the breath of the wind. However, as devotion to Gurudev grew in Kathog and its neighbouring regions, it stirred undercurrents of tension within the confines of the mighty Jwalaji temple nearby, setting the stage for a story of spiritual recognition.
>
> Raji ji recounts: "It is said that there was a pair of khadaus, belonging to a siddh purush, kept in the basement of the Jwalaji temple. These khadaus were so powerful that only someone with immense spiritual strength could take even a single step in them. This became a way for the temple priests to differentiate an evolved spiritualist from a pretender.
>
> "As Gurudev's reputation for miracles grew in Kathog, the priests at Jwalaji decided to put him to the test," he continues. "They led him to the basement, presented him with the khadaus, and asked him to walk in them. With his characteristic calmness, Gurudev put on the khadaus and not only took one step but three, unequivocally proving his spiritual stature."
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13, p.186

*Devobhoomi*: "Land of the Gods." *Siddh purush*: "A realised being who has transcended ordinary physical and mental boundaries through deep spiritual insight and mastery" (footnotes, p.186).
