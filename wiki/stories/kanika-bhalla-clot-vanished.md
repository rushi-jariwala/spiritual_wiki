# Kanika Bhalla Ji — The Clot That Vanished

## Full Narrative

> Kanika Bhalla ji's connection to the sthan emerged from a personal ordeal that became a testimony of spiritual grace.
>
> "Just days after the birth of my first child, I was struck with an intense headache," she recalls. "Confused and in pain, I asked my husband, Dhruv, for a cold bottle of water, which I quickly drank. Moments later, everything went dark. I woke up to find myself in the ICU. I later learnt that I had suffered an epileptic seizure the previous night. Numerous tests revealed that a clot, likely from my caesarean section, had travelled to my occipital lobe, affecting my vision. The doctors warned that even with medication, I could face paralysis or lasting vision issues, with the worst-case scenario being blindness in one eye". The situation was dire, and everyone was deeply concerned.
>
> "Dhruv consulted Guruji (Gurudev's disciple). After looking at my photo, Guruji asked him to go home to rest and return the next morning. Despite his reservations about leaving me, Dhruv followed Guruji's advice. By the time he returned the next morning, the unimaginable had occurred. The medical team was astonished—I had awakened without complications, and the clot had completely vanished!"
>
> The experience bolstered Kanika ji's faith and reshaped her spiritual journey: "Over a decade has passed since that life-changing event. About ten days after I found out what had happened, I called Guruji to tell him that I wanted to wear a kada as I sought refuge under the umbrella of his grace."
> — Hingori and Kanika Bhalla ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, p.135
