# Kapil Mahtani Ji — The Weight in the London Hotel Room

## Full Narrative

> Kapil Mahtani ji, a sevadaar at the Mumbai sthan, recounts an insightful experience from a family trip to London in 2019, which affirms the protective power of mantras: "Upon our arrival at the hotel, we were initially accommodated in a rather cramped room. Dissatisfied, I requested a larger space, and the hotel staff obliged by relocating us. However, I was struck by an unsettling heaviness around my head when we stepped into the new room."
>
> This discomfort escalated into a tangible force pressing down on him during the night, rendering him immobile. "I was filled with the realisation that I was facing a spiritual attack," he says. "Instinctively, I began to recite the Mahagayatri mantra mentally, and within moments, the oppressive weight lifted, freeing me from its hold. This experience was a lesson in the mantra's efficacy as a protection against unseen energies."
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11, p.157

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11 (Sacred Syllables), p.157.
