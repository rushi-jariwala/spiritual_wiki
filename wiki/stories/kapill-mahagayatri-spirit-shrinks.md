# Kapill Ji — The Spirit That Shrank to a Foot

## Full Narrative

> Kapill ji's story resonates with the Mahagayatri mantra's remarkable efficacy: "Sixteen years ago, my gurubhai and I went to the house of a family that visits the sthan and does seva. Guruji (Gurudev's disciple) had instructed us to clear the negative energies from their home. Unexpectedly, without prior discussion, we closed our eyes and began chanting the Mahagayatri mantra. A female figure emerged in our vision. As we continued chanting, she shrank in size, transitioning from a five-foot presence to a mere foot, before vanishing entirely."
>
> What followed next was even more astonishing. "Ordinarily, we use the Chamunda mantra for such tasks," he acknowledges. "Yet, that day, guided seemingly by shared instinct and purpose, we found ourselves chanting the Mahagayatri mantra. Remarkably, upon reflecting on the event after we left the house, my gurubhai and I discovered we had both visualised the same spirit going through the same change at the same time!"
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11, p.157

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11 (Sacred Syllables), p.157.

## Notes

- "Ordinarily, we use the Chamunda mantra for such tasks" — confirms the Chamunda as the sthans' usual mantra for clearing spirits (Darius Moos ji, Ridhim ji, Rajeev Hazarat ji in [[concepts/mantra-vidya]]).
- "Guruji (Gurudev's disciple)" is the same unnamed figure of [[stories/kapill-dream-fourth-eye-gurubhai]].
