# The Old Man Who Carried His Canes Home — Kathog

## Full Narrative

> The residents of Kathog witnessed many extraordinary occurrences, including one that continues to astonish all who hear it. The event centres around an older man, bent with age and relying on two canes to support his frail form, who sought an audience with Gurudev. Despite his aged and frail appearance, Gurudev inquired, "Baba, what if I heal your back?" The elderly man, worn by the passage of time and perhaps sceptical of the possibility of healing, replied matter-of-factly, "I'm too old to be healed". Unfazed by the response, Gurudev took a glass of jal, sprinkled a few drops on the older man, and gave him the remainder to drink. Then, he straightened the man's spine with a hand on his back and the other on his chest and asked him to take a few steps.
>
> Initially, the older man was apprehensive, afraid of falling without the support of his canes. But Gurudev, with reassuring confidence, set the canes aside and assisted him in walking. Once the man had taken a few steps, Gurudev encouraged him further, saying, "Baba, take these canes on your shoulder and go home."
>
> To the astonishment of those present, the man did what Gurudev instructed. He walked, carrying the canes on his shoulder, the stoop in his back noticeably diminished. The onlookers, including Santosh ji, were left amazed, looking at the transformed man as if they had seen a ghost.
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13, pp.184–185

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13 — "Training Trails," pp.184–185 (Kathog, Himachal Pradesh, 1976; witnessed by Santosh ji). *The Guru of Gurus* records the same healing in one line: "He placed his hands on a man with a bent spine and straightened it in minutes" (see [[entities/mahaguru]], Kathog).
