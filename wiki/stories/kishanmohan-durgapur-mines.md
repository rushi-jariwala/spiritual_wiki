# Kishanmohan Ji — Mantra-Charged Mustard Seeds Around the Durgapur Mines

## Full Narrative

> Kishanmohan ji has dedicated over three decades to the practice of mantras. His expertise is primarily used to address the unique and challenging issues faced by Durgapur's local community. Despite the town's modernity, it grapples with problems linked to witchcraft and black magic. He notes, "With Gurudev's guidance, we have harnessed the power of mantras to shield people from diverse problems, achieving a 75 to 80% success rate."
>
> He elaborates on a particular concern: the local mines—notorious for their hazards, including frequent accidents, flooding, and deadly gas emissions. Gurudev theorised that the miners' constant battles against nature's caprices within these mines might inadvertently invite negative energies, escalating the risk of accidents and mishaps.
>
> Recognising the seriousness of the situation and aware that many of his followers worked as miners, Kishanmohan ji took swift action. Heeding Gurudev's advice, he encircled the mines, scattering mustard seeds energised with the Mahagayatri mantra to ward off negative energies. Furthermore, he gave the miners cloves and cardamoms that had been blessed with the mantra. This unique approach—combining the power of mantra-charged mustard seeds and blessed spices—notably improved the safety of the miners in their underground activities.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.11, p.158
