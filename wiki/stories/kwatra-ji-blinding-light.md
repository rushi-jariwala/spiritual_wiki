# Kwatra Ji Collapses in Blinding Light

**Source:** *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3 — Impossible Is Possible, p.34–35

## Full Narrative

> Kwatra ji, a resident of Gurgaon, witnessed a spectacle of light that turned him from a sceptic to a believer. He recalls: "It was 3 am on Mahashivratri. When I arrived at the sthan, there was a long queue to see Gurudev. When I finally met him, I told him I would bow at his feet if he could persuade me that he is my guru. Gurudev sat in padmasana with his hands raised. The room instantly filled with dazzling light as if hundreds of suns had risen! I could not stand how bright it was and collapsed at his feet. He blessed me by placing both hands on my head. I am not sure how long I was lying at his feet, but the memory of the incident gives me goosebumps!"
> — Kwatra ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.34–35

## Context

This incident took place on Mahashivratri — a night of elevated spiritual intensity in Gurudev's calendar — at 3 am, when queues at the sthan stretched for hours. Kwatra ji came with a condition: he would accept Gurudev as his guru only upon proof. The proof arrived not through argument but through direct overwhelming experience. His collapse and the laying of both hands on his head echo the "both hands" as the most powerful blessing form noted by Hingori in [[stories/gurudev-blessing-variations]].

The story is one of several in *Witnessing Greatness* Ch.3 where scepticism is dissolved not by discourse but by direct encounter with the mahaguru's radiant form. The account was narrated a few years after Shambhu ji had witnessed Gurudev's five-headed form — see [[stories/shambhuji-five-headed-form]].
