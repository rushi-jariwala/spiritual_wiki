# Malhotra Ji's Astral Meeting and the Yog Dand Imprint

## Full Narrative

> Ashok ji shares an account from RC Malhotra ji: "Malhotra ji found himself with Gurudev and Buddhe Baba in what seemed to be an astral meeting. During the meeting, Malhotra ji expressed his opinions, which seemed to upset Buddhe Baba. Holding a yog dand,¹ Buddhe Baba struck Malhotra ji on his back. When Malhotra ji awoke in the morning, what he had thought was only a whack in a dream left him with real pain in that same spot. Upon having someone examine his back, they discovered an imprint resembling that of a yog dand!"
>
> When Ashok ji asked about the surreal experience of an ethereal beating manifesting physically, Malhotra ji responded cryptically, emphasising the complexities of such spiritual phenomena.
>
> ¹ An ancient tool, conceived by yogis, crafted specifically to manipulate the flow of breath.
>
> — Ashok Bhalla ji (narrating RC Malhotra ji's account), *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.2, p.30

> [!tip] The chapter uses this account and [[stories/hingori-fan-ankle]] to establish a principle: physical imprints from astral-plane encounters are not anomalous. The spirit body and the physical body share a feedback channel — sufficiently forceful contact in the astral leaves marks in the physical. This is consistent with the teaching in [[concepts/astral-travel]] that the spirit body is not an inert projection but a real body of subtler matter.

> [!tip] Buddhe Baba's disciplinary stance here — physically striking Malhotra ji for expressing unwelcome opinions — reveals a quality of the guide that the chapter's other accounts do not: he is not merely benevolent and reassuring. He acts with authority, even severity, when it serves the disciple's evolution.
