# Mataji Discovers Gurudev in Paath

## Full Narrative

> "I had no idea about his spiritual pursuits when we married. One day, I went to talk to him and found him lying motionless on the bed. He was not breathing. I frantically tried to wake him, shaking his hands, feet, and body, but there was no reaction. In a panic, I called my sister-in-law to see what had happened to him. She calmly explained, 'There's no need to be anxious; he is in paath'. Back then, I did not understand what paath meant."
>
> — Mataji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, p.75

> "I discovered later that he frequently meditated near water bodies in his teens. He would spend the entire day meditating, and when he got home, he meditated all night. He embraced a lifestyle marked by austerity and a notable degree of self-reliance. He preferred preparing his meals and would resort to a simple diet like roti with jaggery when vegetables were unavailable. He also visited many saints and shared his thoughts and ideas. That is how he expanded his knowledge."
>
> — Mataji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, pp.75–76

Mataji also describes the gradual unfolding of her own spiritual path — entirely self-directed, with Gurudev never trying to steer her:

> "I felt a deep affinity for the Gayatri mantra. Upon sharing my experiences with Gurudev, he acknowledged the mantra's significance."
>
> — Mataji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, p.76

As her commitment to the Gayatri mantra deepened, Gurudev eventually shared the more powerful Mahagayatri mantra with her — a measured, thoughtful act rather than a sudden transmission.
