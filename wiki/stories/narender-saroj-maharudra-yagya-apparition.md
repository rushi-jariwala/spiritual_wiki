# Gurudev Appears at the Maharudra Yagya

**Source:** *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3 — Impossible Is Possible, p.42–43

## Full Narrative

> In July 1992, Narender ji and his wife Saroj ji organised the Maharudra yagya. During this time, about 16 priests participated in the rituals. A day before the yagya, they were at the sthan in their home when they recall briefly seeing Gurudev in his physical form. On another occasion, they saw a person in all white approaching the yagshaala. Everyone in the family was in agreement that it was Gurudev. A few years later, their daughter noticed Gurudev standing at their sthan in Karnal. Before she could react, he had vanished into thin air.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.42–43

## Context

July 1992 is approximately one year after Gurudev's physical passing. The Maharudra yagya — an elaborate Vedic fire ritual — is among the most auspicious and spiritually charged events in the tradition; its performance by a devoted disciple family would naturally draw the mahaguru's presence. The three separate sightings — at the home sthan the day before, approaching the yagshaala in white, and at Karnal years later — span different members of the family, confirming that multiple independent witnesses observed the same entity. The Karnal appearance includes the characteristic instant dissolution — "vanished into thin air" — that marks many post-mortem sightings.

The names Narender ji and Saroj ji are separately noted as having their records in the Bhrigu Samhita — their family's link to the tradition extends far back in the historical tapestry.
