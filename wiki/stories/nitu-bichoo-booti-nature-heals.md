# Nitu ji and the Bichoo Booti — Nature Has a Cure for Everything

## Full Narrative

> Nitu ji recalls, "One incident during our visit to Daddy's camp in Himachal Pradesh stands out in my memory. While playing near the road, I accidentally brushed against something with my hand as I made way for a herd of passing donkeys. Little did I know then that it was a plant called Bichoo Booti [Poison Ivy]. The brush against this shrub brought intense redness, pain, and swelling to my hand. Unaware of the plant's effects, I rubbed my hand in distress, only worsening the pain. Seeking comfort, I ran to Daddy's room.
>
> "Concerned, Daddy inquired, 'What happened to you, Pandit ji?' I explained the incident through tears, not understanding why he kept asking questions instead of offering immediate healing. But Daddy remained composed, asking for more details and investigating the incident thoroughly. Finally, he suggested, 'Let us go to where this happened.'
>
> "I led Daddy to the spot, hoping for relief from the unbearable pain. He identified the Bichoo Booti as the culprit and pointed to its roots as the cure. He gently applied the plant's roots to my hand, and I felt immediate relief. 'Son, nature has a cure for everything,' he said, 'all you have to know is where to look for it.'"

— Nitu ji (Gurudev's youngest son), *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, p.82
