# Who Is This Om Baba? — Gurudev's Sister Finds Out

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev embodied the essence of selfless service, ensuring that the focus remained on the mission and not the man. While he was unfolding miracles at Kathog, his family in Hariana remained blissfully unaware of his spiritual metamorphosis.
>
> A conversation with a fellow student named Dilbaag, who hailed from Jwalaji, led to his sister's discovery of an 'Om Baba' performing miracles there. Upon Gurudev's return, his sister, fuelled by curiosity, inquired, "Who is this Om Baba who had visited Kathog?" Gurudev questioned how she came to know of him, to which she explained Dilbaag's revelations about a revered sage and the substantial crowd gathering to seek his help and blessings.
>
> The veil lifted, and Gurudev confessed that he was indeed the 'Om Baba' to whom her friend had referred. This admission was a simple acknowledgement for him but a staggering surprise for his sister.
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13, p.187

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13 — "Training Trails," p.187. *The Guru of Gurus* gives the same event in brief: his sisters learned he had become a guru only by chance in 1976, via a classmate who mentioned the 'OM wale baba' at Kathog (see [[entities/mahaguru]], Family).
