# Pappu Ji — The Severed Finger

## Full Narrative

> Pappu ji was part of a group I fondly call the 'Four Musketeers', which included Nikku ji, Gaggu ji, and Bittu ji. Together, they were indispensable to the mahaguru, attending to his daily needs and handling the administrative duties at the sthan. Pappu ji shares a personal story of healing: "I was operating a power press machine when, suddenly, part of my finger was severed. At that moment, I felt nothing; I wasn't even aware my finger had been cut. It was only when I looked down and saw the severed part on the floor that I realised my hand was bleeding profusely. Since I was near Gurudev's sthan in Gurgaon, I went straight to him."
>
> Upon reaching the sthan, Pappu ji describes how the mahaguru tended to him: "Gurudev quietly examined my injured finger, held it in his hands for about half a minute, and simply told me to go home". Astonishingly, this brief encounter led to the complete regeneration of his finger. "Not only did my finger regenerate in some time, but its functioning and mobility are entirely normal."
>
> He reflects on the mahaguru's humble approach: "Gurudev was always understated about his abilities. Often, those he helped didn't realise the extent of his intervention until much later, only connecting the dots after they had healed."
> — Hingori and Pappu ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, p.120
