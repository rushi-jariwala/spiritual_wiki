# Pooja Seth Ji — The Flower in the Coconut

## Full Narrative

> Pooja Seth ji has dedicated years to seva, accruing significant spiritual depth. She shares a touching experience, illustrating the mahaguru's enduring impact on both his disciples and those they serve.
>
> She begins, "A few days before Guru Purnima, I had a dream of Gurudev in which he gave me a coconut that had a flower inside. He imparted a clear instruction: the flower would be given to a woman who could not conceive a child.
>
> "On Guru Purnima, it is a practice for disciples to get coconuts blessed by their guru—a ritual that symbolises the surrender of one's ego. When I shared my dream with Guruji (Gurudev's disciple), he advised me to reserve the blessed coconut for someone in need. I placed it in my home temple, and to my amazement, a flower miraculously bloomed under the cloth covering the coconut. After discussing this development with Guruji, he pointed me towards a woman who had been visiting the sthan, struggling for years to conceive. I gifted her the flower. It was not long before joyful news reached me—she was pregnant. Her journey of motherhood began with this child, and she had three or four more children in the years that followed."
>
> Pooja ji reveals the lasting impact of this experience, "For several years following this event, I discovered fresh flowers sprouting from the coconuts that Guruji had blessed for me each Guru Purnima. Since then, we have made it a practice to offer these white coconut flowers to women who visit the sthan with infertility issues."
> — Hingori and Pooja Seth ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.131–132
