# Pradeep Ji's Dattatreya Vision and the Shiv Temple on the Hill

## Full Narrative

> During one of his meditative sessions, Pradeep Sethi ji had a vision in which he saw the deity Dattatreya, enveloped in brilliant white light, standing beside a tree with his white dog and cow. He acknowledges, "Such visions are not uncommon on the spiritual journey, so I did not dwell much on it at the time."
>
> Later, upon his return to Mumbai after a stint in Delhi, the idea of building a Shiv temple started to take root in his thoughts. In his quest for the perfect location, he chanced upon a site with an ancient Shivling atop a hill. An intuitive feeling assured him that this was the preordained spot. However, just as he agreed on a price with the land broker, the broker unexpectedly backed out, causing the deal to fall through.
>
> A few months following the initial setbacks, Pradeep ji was pleasantly surprised by a call from Mrs Kapoor, a devotee from Delhi. He recalls, "She excitedly recounted a dream in which she saw me opening a temple on a hill and offering jalebis to the crowd". A mere ten minutes after their conversation concluded, the broker called and agreed to proceed with the property transaction, marking an unexpected turn of events.
>
> During a subsequent visit to the site with a contractor, Pradeep ji made a remarkable discovery. He found an Audumbar (Ficus racemosa) tree on the premises, a tree sacred to Dattatreya and similar to the one he had seen in his vision. This reinforced his belief that a divine force was guiding his endeavour to establish the temple.
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.8, pp.99–100
