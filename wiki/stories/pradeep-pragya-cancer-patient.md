# Pradeep ji — Service Before Self, Daughter Healed After

## Full Narrative

> The phrase 'Service before self' may seem overused to some, but it was the bedrock of Gurudev's philosophy. He often said, "Serve my purpose, and I will fulfil yours."
>
> Pradeep ji faced a moral quandary when his daughter, Pragya, sustained a severe head injury from a fall, leaving her semi-conscious. He had previously committed to visiting a young cancer patient that very day — a commitment made all the more poignant because the boy's father had dreamt of Pradeep ji healing his son.
>
> Faced with the agonising choice of either rushing his daughter to Gurudev for immediate healing or honouring his promise to the cancer patient's father, Pradeep ji prioritised his spiritual duty over personal urgency and first visited the young cancer patient.
>
> Subsequently, Pradeep ji and his daughter travelled to Gurgaon to seek Gurudev's intervention. Upon their arrival, Gurudev greeted them with a knowing smile. He blessed Pragya and began her path to recovery.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.5, pp.67–68
