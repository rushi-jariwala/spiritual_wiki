# Pradeep Ji — Sages at Gurudev's Chautha and Sai Baba's Promise

## Full Narrative

> Narinder ji recounts a conversation in which the mahaguru revealed the profound connection he shares with Sai Baba of Shirdi. He remembers Gurudev saying, "When I travel astrally from Gurgaon to Mumbai, I always pause at two places—Ajmer Sharif and Shirdi. My bond with Shirdi Sai runs deep."
>
> Pradeep ji witnessed an extraordinary sight during Gurudev's chautha ceremony that affirms this alliance. He shares, "It was late afternoon. The place was teeming with mourners. Among them, I spotted a congregation of hundreds of ancient sages sitting before me. And there, in their centre, was Sai Baba of Shirdi. I saw this sight with my eyes wide open!"
>
> This experience left Pradeep ji deeply reflective. "I wondered what the scene meant. Six months later, clarity came through a dream. Gurudev came to me and said, 'When a doctor dies, don't other doctors gather to remember him? If a tradesperson passes, don't his peers show up to say goodbye? Likewise, when spiritual beings leave the earthly realm, those connected to them on the spiritual path come to pay their respects.'"
>
> Pradeep ji continues, "Another event unfolded shortly after Gurudev's passing. Around seven to eight months into my seva at my building in Mumbai, between 15 to 20 neighbours voiced their displeasure. They found the weekly Thursday gatherings at the sthan disruptive and called for their discontinuation. In the past, I would always turn to Gurudev for guidance during challenging times. However, without him by my side, I felt adrift. After finishing my seva, I withdrew to my room. I questioned why others would be offended when all my actions stemmed from devotion to my guru. To my astonishment, as I looked up, the vision of Sai Baba of Shirdi appeared before me. Draped in a green headscarf, he asked if I was afraid. He revealed that he had been pelted with stones during his time. After a moment's pause, he comforted me, saying, 'From this day forward, no one shall bother you during seva'. Three decades have rolled by, and, staying true to his words, my seva remains undisturbed."
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.8, pp.104–105
