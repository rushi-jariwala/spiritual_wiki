# Pravesh Kapoor Ji — 80 Kilometres on an Empty Tank

## Full Narrative

> Pravesh Kapoor ji, who accompanied Gurudev on many trips across India, shares another intriguing tale of the mahaguru's mastery over the elements: "Once, the petrol in my car ran out completely. Gurudev looked at me and said, 'Son, open the petrol tank'. I did as he asked, and then he told me to close the lid."
>
> "He then calmly instructed us to start the car," Parvesh ji continues. "We did, and to our amazement, the car started, and we drove on. We never stopped for petrol after that. We drove back to Delhi, and only once we were there did we finally fill up the tank."
>
> When asked how far they had travelled without any petrol, Parvesh ji replies, "At least 80 to 85 kilometres on an empty tank!"
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, pp.176–177

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony), pp.176–177.
