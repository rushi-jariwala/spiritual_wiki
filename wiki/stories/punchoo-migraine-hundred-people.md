# Punchoo Ji — Help a Hundred People

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev's teachings went beyond the mundane, emphasising empowerment and the importance of paying it forward. Punchoo ji and Giri ji's sister's experiences serve as prime examples, showcasing his holistic approach.
>
> "I was no stranger to the throbbing pain of migraines," Punchoo ji explains. "One day, my maternal uncle approached Gurudev on my behalf. Rather than directly curing me, Gurudev suggested, 'Help a hundred people find relief from their headache, and your migraines will go away'. Taking his advice to heart, I began practising the technique he had taught me.
>
> "During a school trip to Jaipur, a classmate struggled with a headache. I gently pressed her forehead with my thumb and forefinger like Gurudev had shown me. To her and my amazement, her pain vanished instantly! Word spread, and soon, others lined up for their turn. It became a light-hearted joke—any headache sufferer knew where to get relief. It felt as though there was magic in my fingers!"
>
> The magic in Punchoo ji's fingers was more than just a remedy; it symbolised the mahaguru's ability to transform individuals into channels of his healing grace.
> — Hingori and Punchoo ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.128–129
