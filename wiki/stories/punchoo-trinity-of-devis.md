# Punchoo Ji Is Introduced to the Trinity of Devis

## Full Narrative

> Punchoo ji joined the mahaguru's flock as a young girl. Born to a disciple, Rajpal ji, she later married another disciple, Pradeep ji, making her part of an intricate web of relationships within Gurudev's spiritual family. Her experiences, like many within the group, often blur the lines between the tangible and the mystical.
>
> She shares a vivid dream: "I found myself perched high above, in a place untouched by clouds. Beneath me were interlacing red pipes hovering in the air. Beside me, defying gravity, stood Gurudev, oddly casual in jeans and a T-shirt, nonchalantly twirling car keys. My gaze shifted to three radiant women approaching. The first, clad in a red sari, had fair skin and traditional gold jewellery, accentuating her plain appearance. Trailing behind her was a striking beauty, dressed in an elegant black sari adorned with golden paisley motifs, her long, silky black hair flowing behind her. The third followed them in a delicate peach sari with a golden design. Their movement was graceful and fluid, more of a glide than a walk. As they neared, they paid their respects, hinting to Gurudev that he should have summoned them. Gurudev smiled, mentioning he had brought me along especially to introduce me to them. Deep down, I recognised them as the trinity of devis: Laxmi ji, Parvati ji, and Saraswati ji."
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.8, p.102
