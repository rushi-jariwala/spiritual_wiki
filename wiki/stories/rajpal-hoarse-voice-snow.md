# Rajpal Ji's Hoarse Voice — Ego in Seva

## Full Narrative (*The Guru of Gurus*, p.228)

> To teach Rajpal ji a lesson for showing off his singing skills during seva, Gurudev made his voice so hoarse that he could hardly utter an understandable word. A few months later, when he again sought forgiveness from his guru, Gurudev asked him to eat a ball of snow to reclaim his vocals. Rajpal picked up some snow from the ground he was standing on and ate it. The cure was almost instantaneous.
> — *The Guru of Gurus*, p.228

## Guru Sutra Version (Ch.12, pp.122–124)

*Guru Sutra* gives a more detailed telling — including the original context of the singing, the duration of the correction, and the exact setting of the cure:

> Rajpalji is one of Gurudev's older disciples who was, and continues to be obsessed with him. In the early years of discipleship, I believe, Rajpalji did something that could probably be termed as guru awelna. Like me, he too was punished.
>
> One day, as he sat in the sthan, singing songs (probably with the intention of impressing the visitors), Gurudev looked at him and said, "Alright son, let me see how you sing again". Within no time, Rajpalji's throat got sore and remained that way for over a year. During a visit to Shimla with Gurudev, he pleaded for a cure. Gurudev asked him to pick snow off the ground and eat it. Rajpalji did as told and instantly, his throat was normal again.
> — Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.12, pp.122–124

## Source

- *The Guru of Gurus*, Healing — Modes of Healing, p.228

## Notes

The *Guru Sutra* version explicitly frames this as [[concepts/guru-awelna]], placing it in a sequence of awelna examples. Key additions over the *Guru of Gurus* account:

- The specific trigger: singing at the sthan "probably with the intention of impressing the visitors" — the ego-motivation is named
- Gurudev's response in the moment: the precise and understated "Alright son, let me see how you sing again" — not a reprimand but a seemingly encouraging sentence that became the correction
- Duration: the sore throat remained for **over a year**, not just months
- The cure was given at **Shimla**, during a visit with Gurudev — Rajpalji pleaded at that time

The cure remains as unconventional as in the earlier telling: snow off the ground, instant restoration. The correction was precise and targeted; the cure was equally precise once genuine contrition was established.
