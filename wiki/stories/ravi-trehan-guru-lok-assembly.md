# Ravi Trehan Ji — An Assembly of Mahagurus in Guru Lok

## Full Narrative

> The tales of Gurudev's astral voyages spark wonder and awe. If penned, the sheer volume and depth of these experiences would dwarf the expansive records of the Encyclopaedia Britannica.
>
> Ravi Trehan ji's experiential testimony adds to this anthology: "I cannot assert it with absolute certainty, as I don't have complete proof, but the impression I received strongly suggested that I astrally travelled to Guru Lok with Gurudev."
>
> When asked about the experience, he elaborates, "In that state, I felt the presence of ancient mahagurus. Their appearances matched the ones we see in photographs—long-haired and bearded, dressed in traditional attire. It was like being in an assembly. I recall seeing beings such as Brahmrishi Vishwamitra and Guru Vashisth. It seemed as though all these esteemed sages were gathered there."
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, p.174

The book's footnote on Guru Lok: "A spiritual realm or plane of existence in Hindu and Sikh traditions, believed to be inhabited by enlightened souls and spiritual masters, including mahagurus. It is considered a place of ultimate peace and divine wisdom, where souls reside in a state of bliss and are free from the cycle of birth and death" (p.174).

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony), p.174 (illustration: "Ravi Trehan ji sees an assembly of mahagurus in Guru Lok," p.175).
