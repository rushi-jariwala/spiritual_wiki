# Renu Ji's Childhood Encounter with Buddhe Baba

## Full Narrative

> "My first encounter with Buddhe Baba was during my childhood at our home in Shivpuri, Gurgaon. Until that day, I had only heard about him. Daddy would ask us to silently share any deep-seated wish with Buddhe Baba at the sthan," she shares, her voice tinged with nostalgia. "I was home alone, sick with a fever, while Mummy and Daddy were at work. Suddenly, a man dressed in white appeared from the storeroom. He had long white hair and a flowing beard. I don't recall his height, but his upright posture made him seem imposing. Terrified, I covered myself with a blanket, hoping he wouldn't see me. When Mummy returned, I clung to her, saying, 'I am scared.' She reassured me, 'Why are you afraid? This is our home, and we have the sthan here. Chant your mantra'. I told her that even while chanting, I felt his presence linger. 'That was Buddhe Baba,' Mummy calmly assured me, 'He watches over this sthan and all of us.'"
>
> — Renu ji (Gurudev's eldest daughter), *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.2, p.28
