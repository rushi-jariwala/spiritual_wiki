# Renu ji Confronts Gurudev — Love Is Like Radiation

## Full Narrative

> As the crowd seeking his help and blessings swelled outside their home, young Renu ji experienced a growing sense of distance from her father. Their moments together grew increasingly scarce due to the influx of visitors. There were stretches, sometimes 10 to 15 days or even an entire month, when they barely exchanged words or shared time. This situation weighed heavily on her, especially when she heard her classmates happily sharing stories about spending quality time with their fathers.
>
> "I mustered the courage one day to tell him that he did not have time for us and that Mummy was everything to us," she admits. "I told him we could not survive without her. He locked his stare on me for two minutes. I expected him to be annoyed with me. Instead, he said, 'I do not know how to convey affection like your mother. One day you will realise, and then you will understand what true love is.'"
>
> Explaining the meaning of true love to a young child can be like describing a colour they have never seen. Love is more than an emotion. It is like radiation permeating everything, unrestricted by boundaries. It should envelop everyone like a fragrance, not just a select few. Reflecting on this, Renu ji admits, "It was only in the aftermath of his passing that I began to grasp the depth of his concept of love. Perhaps, had I realised it while he was in his physical form, I would not have felt like I do now. I now understand what guru kripa is, how the guru imparts his wisdom, and how his influence encompasses all."

— Renu ji and Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.6, pp.80–81
