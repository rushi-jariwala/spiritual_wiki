# Renu Ji at Renuka — Parshuram Ji Tests Gurudev's Family Ties

## Full Narrative

> When Gurudev first arrived in Renuka, he instructed his disciples not to acknowledge him as their guru. He aimed to keep his identity hidden until he had established a significant spiritual alliance with Parshuram ji.
>
> Many spiritual luminaries often test each other's spirituality before recognising each other formally. Bittu ji shares a story involving Renu ji, who was seriously ill in Gurgaon and wished to see her father, who was then in Renuka: "Mataji, along with Subbhash ji and Pal ji, brought her to Renuka to meet Gurudev. Upon their arrival, Gurudev was visibly displeased. Mataji explained, 'This wasn't solely my decision; your daughter, who is ill, wanted to see you.' Gurudev responded sternly, 'If it is her destiny to die, we must accept it.' He then immediately instructed them to return to Gurgaon."
>
> As evening neared, Bittu courageously asked if they could spend the night at Bayri. Gurudev consented but imposed one condition: they had to leave before he woke up the following day. Later, Gurudev revealed that Parshuram ji was testing him to assess his emotional ties to his family. Gurudev demonstrated his detachment by meeting this challenge, earning Parshuram ji's approval. This event signified the beginning of a lasting partnership between him and the immortal one.
>
> Gurudev utilised his camps to establish several spiritual alliances, not only with the venerable Parshuram ji and his mother, Renuka ji, but also with other devi-devtas and many other spiritual entities yet to be unveiled.
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13, p.189
