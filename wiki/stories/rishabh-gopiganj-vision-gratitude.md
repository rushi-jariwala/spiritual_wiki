# Rishabh Manaktala Ji — "At Least I Will Have Another Chance to See You"

## Full Narrative

> Rishabh Manaktala ji, a dedicated sevadaar from Mumbai, recounts a transformative experience from the early days of seva at the sthan in Gopiganj, a village near Benaras.
>
> "A young man came to Guruji (Gurudev's disciple) with a concerning loss of vision," Rishabh ji begins. "Guruji said, 'Let's see what we can do for you today'. After the initial healing session, the young man noted some improvement, but his vision was still blurred. Guruji's response was gentle yet realistic, 'This is the best we can do for you today.'"
>
> Rishabh ji then captures a moment of pure acceptance: "With a heartfelt smile, the man said, 'That is okay. At least I will have another chance to see you'. His contentment and positive outlook inspired Guruji to extend the healing process."
>
> The story culminates in a remarkable turnaround: "By the end of the day, the man had regained most of his vision. This outcome resulted from the sthan's energy, the guru's grace, and the young man's genuine gratitude and acceptance. His willingness to embrace whatever grace was bestowed, without demanding a complete cure, was pivotal."
> — Hingori and Rishabh Manaktala ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, p.133
