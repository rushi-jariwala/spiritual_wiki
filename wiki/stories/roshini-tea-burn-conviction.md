# "I Am Gurudev's Daughter" — Roshini Ji's Tea Burn

## Full Narrative

> Roshini ji shares a story demonstrating the mahaguru's remarkable influence over the elements, transcending his physical presence. "It was a typical Sunday seva at the Mumbai sthan, and I was serving tea to the visitors. While conversing with a visitor, I accidentally spilt hot tea on my hand, causing immediate pain and visible redness. My hand began to sting sharply and turned a deep red," she recalls.
>
> Caught up in her duties, Roshini ji did not immediately attend to her burn, and the pain and redness worsened. It was after she managed to rinse her hand under cold water a few minutes later that a thought struck her. "I am Gurudev's daughter. He mastered control over fire, so my hand will be fine," she remembers thinking. Miraculously, within moments of this realisation, "The pain subsided, the redness disappeared, and there were no traces of the burn," she describes, amazed at the healing that followed her conviction.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, p.169
