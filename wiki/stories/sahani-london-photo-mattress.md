# Sahani Ji — The Photograph Under the Mattress

## Full Narrative

> Healing is a deep science involving multiple levels of a healer's consciousness. This intricate interplay is evident in a story shared by a devotee, Sahani ji, about a young man from London who needed frequent bone marrow transplants.
>
> "The young man's mother and uncle came to me, hoping I could seek Gurudev's intervention on their behalf," he recalls. "I showed Gurudev the young man's photo and explained his situation, highlighting his responsibilities as a father to two young children. Gurudev gave a hopeful assurance."
>
> When Gurudev met the young man's uncle, he inquired whether the young man would be open to taking blessed cloves, cardamoms, and jal. The uncle responded that he would consult with his nephew on this matter. Meanwhile, Gurudev placed the young man's photograph under his bed mattress, a gesture intended to alleviate the difficulties faced by the person in the image. Sahani ji elaborates, "Photos placed under Gurudev's mattress were said to grant individuals either healing or an extension of life. Remarkably, the photographs of approximately 8,000 to 10,000 individuals lay beneath that mattress, all seeking Gurudev's intervention for their unique challenges."
>
> "However, a week later, upon discovering that the young man was hesitant to take jal, Gurudev expressed his disappointment. He then instructed Puran ji and me to remove the young man's photo from under the mattress. But despite our best efforts, we could not find it. And surprisingly, the young man's health improved significantly."
>
> This story reveals a complex insight: on the one hand, Gurudev's guru form offered hope and promised healing. On the other hand, his physical form displayed irritability when the young man did not follow his instructions. While Gurudev's physical form reacted to the young man's non-compliance, his guru form, operating on a higher plane, offered unconditional healing regardless of whether his advice was heeded.
> — Hingori and Sahani ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.127–128

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, "Sage of Solace," pp.127–128
