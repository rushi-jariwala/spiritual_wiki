# Santlal Ji's Astral Journey Home — Through the Grills

## Full Narrative

> Santlal ji shares a remarkable tale of how, during a camp, the mahaguru empowered him and two of his gurubhais to leave their physical forms and embark on an astral journey back to their homes.
>
> He recalls, "We locked our doors, lit a dhoop, lay down, and started doing our paath. We then travelled to our respective homes in spirit form. My task was to enter my home through the grills and perform puja, strictly adhering to Gurudev's instructions not to interact with family members or drink water. I instinctively called out to my wife but then recalled Gurudev's words. Though I was curious about my children's well-being, I refrained from further contact, respecting his instructions.
>
> "After our astral travels, Gurudev's grace skillfully guided Bakshi ji and me back to our physical forms. Without his divine intervention, how would we have known the way to exit and re-enter our bodies, particularly when encased by grills?"
>
> However, the third astral voyager, Amichand ji, found himself adrift and did not return. Gurudev had to travel astrally to bring him back to his body.
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, pp.173–174

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony), pp.173–174.

## Notes

- *The Guru of Gurus* (Supernature, p.247) summarises the same event as navigation training: visit your homes, do not talk to your families, return to your bodies before dawn; Amichand ji lost his way and had to be fetched. Ch.12 adds Santlal ji's own voice — the locked doors and dhoop, entering "through the grills," performing puja, the instruction not to drink water, and calling out to his wife before checking himself.
- The instruction not to interact recurs in [[stories/guddan-astral-visit-dying-mother]] ("I reached her but did not touch her").
