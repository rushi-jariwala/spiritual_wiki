# Santlal Ji's Journey From Gangowal to Gurudev — Nine Lifetimes of Waiting

## Full Narrative

**Source:** Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.4, pp.52–53

Santlal ji's transformation is described as one of stark metamorphosis: from bureaucratic arrogance to spiritual enlightenment. Initially far from what one might consider a potential disciple, he evolved into an exemplar of disciplehood.

### The Gangowal Sadhu

Santlal ji narrates his pursuit of a guru:

> "In the heart of Punjab lies Gangowal, where I heard of a wise sadhu. My first encounter with him was anticlimactic; he asked me to return the next day. Disappointed but persistent, I requested an early meeting, only to be asked to return every day for five days. I waited for hours in long queues to meet him. On the fifth day, the sadhu confessed that he could not take me under his wing, for my spiritual allegiance lay elsewhere — with someone present at the time in the same town, no less!"
> — Santlal ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.4, p.52

The sadhu who could not take Santlal ji as his disciple was standing in the same town as Gurudev at that very moment — a coincidence that was no coincidence at all.

### The Midnight Meeting in Gurgaon

Years passed before fate guided Santlal ji to the guru he sought. A business journey brought him to Gurgaon, where chance encounters ushered him toward the mahaguru. They met at **midnight**. Gurudev's simple *lungi*-clad appearance took Santlal ji aback, challenging his preconceived notions of a guru's grandeur:

> "I was sceptical because I had trouble believing that a man so simple could be a guru. I asked him if he had visited Gangowal in 1960, and he said he had. I wondered whom he had met, and he perfectly described the sadhu I had met."
> — Santlal ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.4, p.53

### What Gurudev Revealed

During their first meeting, Gurudev shared three revelations:

1. **On the nature of the guru's selection**: the journey to finding a guru is not driven by the disciple's search but by the guru's intuitive selection of his disciples.
2. **The depth of past-life bonds**: the remarkable breadth of his bond with Santlal ji extended across **nine previous incarnations**.
3. **A mother's blessings intervene**: the blessings Santlal ji's mother had bestowed upon her son had brought forward their destined encounter by **two years**.
