# Santoshji at Kathok — Recruited on the Spot

**Source:** Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, pp.129–130

> Santoshji was caught unaware when Gurudev asked him to do seva for people who were waiting in long queues at a campsite in Kathok, a one-horse town in Himachal Pradesh. Since he had no clue what to do, Gurudev asked him to distribute jal to the people and sprinkle some over their heads. He indicated that people would get well by this act, and amazingly, many did. I empathise with you, the reader, if you find this hard to swallow, but I want to present things as they were and not dressed up to sound credible.

**Context:** Kathok camp, Himachal Pradesh. Santoshji had received no prior training or preparation; he was assigned healing seva without warning. Gurudev had the ability to give a potential disciple the power to heal within seconds — provided the matching destiny was present. This incident is one of Hingori's clearest illustrations that the operative agent in healing is the guru's intent and the disciple's destiny, not the disciple's trained competence. See [[concepts/intent-as-healing]], [[concepts/guru-tattva]].

The story also appears in [[concepts/transmission-of-power]] as an example of instantaneous energy bestowal.

## *Witnessing Greatness* Version (Ch.13, pp.183–186)

> Santosh ji, a bodybuilder and physical education instructor from Nadaun—just 17 kilometres from Kathog—became captivated by the mahaguru almost instantly. "I met Gurudev in the summer of 1976 when my health was declining," he recalls. "In our first meeting, he revealed the OM and trishul symbols on his hand. Three days later, he summoned me and said, 'Son, allow me to guide you on your path of evolution'. He then bestowed upon me a kada and mantra."
>
> "A few weeks later, I visited the sthan at Shivpuri," he continues. "However, when I arrived, I found that Gurudev was not there. I entered the sthan room and lit a dhoop. It was then that I happened to glance at my hands. To my astonishment, I saw the symbol of the OM in my right hand, mirroring the one I had seen in Gurudev's hand. I also noticed the symbol of a trishul in my left hand. Though startled by the sight, I understood it was Gurudev's blessings manifesting."
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13, pp.183–184

On the seva itself:

> The healing wonders witnessed at Kathog led to faith blossoming and spiritual destinies unfolding. Shambhu ji, Suresh ji, and Santosh ji found themselves converging on purpose. Entrusted by Gurudev with seva, they were armed with merely a glass of water he had sipped and a simple directive: touch where there is pain and trust in the unseen power of my guidance. Embracing their task amidst scepticism, they witnessed the miraculous—each person they touched was healed, a powerful testament to Gurudev's ability to make others the medium of his power.
>
> — *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.13, pp.185–186
