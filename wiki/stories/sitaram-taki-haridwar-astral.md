# Sitaram Taki Witnesses Gurudev's Astral Departure at Haridwar

## Full Narrative

> Sometime in the 1970s, Sitaram Taki ji accompanied Gurudev to Haridwar. One night, the mahaguru asked him to press his feet. He then told Sitaram ji that he should hold on to him and accompany him on an astral journey when he left his body. Soon after, the mahaguru wrapped a sheet around himself and began his paath. What happened next put the fear of god in Sitaram ji. He saw Gurudev wrapped in a sheet emerge from his body while an identical physical form also wrapped in a sheet remained seated on the reclining chair. Sitaram ji was so spooked by what he saw that he wet his pants! After being paralysed with fear for what seemed like an eternity, he felt Gurudev's astral form return to the room and merge with the body seated on the chair.
> — Hingori, *The Guru of Gurus*, pp.98–99


## *Witnessing Greatness* Version (Ch.12, p.173)

> Sitaram Taki ji was in for another shock when he accompanied Gurudev to Haridwar. Before beginning his paath, the mahaguru invited Sitaram ji to hold his legs and join him on an astral journey. As Gurudev entered a trance-like state, his ethereal double began to emerge from his body. Overwhelmed with fear at the sight, Sitaram ji inadvertently released his grip on Gurudev's legs and wet his pants. He remained frozen with fear till Gurudev's astral form returned and merged into his physical form, concluding this extraordinary experience.
>
> My experience mirrored Sitaram ji's. Once, as Gurudev prepared for paath in Gurgaon, he beckoned me to hold his feet, promising a celestial tour of the planets. Yet, as he started to convulse, signalling the onset of his astral departure, my nerves betrayed me. I, too, let go, forfeiting the opportunity to journey the cosmos alongside the mahaguru.
> — Hingori, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.12, p.173

Ch.12 makes explicit what *The Guru of Gurus* leaves implicit: Sitaram ji **released his grip** on Gurudev's legs — and so did not go. Hingori adds his own parallel failure in Gurgaon. See [[synthesis/missed-astral-invitations]].
