# Hostile Temple Near the New Sthan — Resolved with Sweetmeats

**Source:** Hingori, *Guru Sutra*, Ch.13, pp.141–142

> When we opened a sthan on a hill station, I felt the atmosphere there was heavily laden with negativity. On inquiring if there was a temple nearby, I was informed that there was in fact a small but popular temple, a couple metres away. I sent some senior disciples to offer sweetmeats at the temple to assure the deity that we came as friends and not enemies. They communicated to the deity that they would send people with offerings and the temple should not be an obstacle to our seva. Ever since, the atmosphere at that sthan is full of positive energy.

**Context:** On opening a sthan at an unnamed hill station, Hingori perceived the atmosphere as heavily laden with negativity. Diagnosis: a small but popular temple metres away, whose accumulated energy felt threatened by the new spiritual establishment. Rather than confronting or suppressing the temple's energy, Hingori chose diplomacy — senior disciples brought sweetmeats and communicated goodwill to the deity, explicitly acknowledging the temple's presence and committing to send worshippers.

The resolution was immediate: the atmosphere cleared. This is the only account in the corpus of proactive negotiation with a hostile temple energy — demonstrating that the correct response to territorial spiritual forces is deference, not assertion.

Contrast with [[stories/hingori-deity-attack-sleep]], where a different hostile temple deity attacked Hingori while sleeping, and he chose to ignore (not engage) the attack.
