# Virender Ji's Wife — "Either Heal Her or Let Her Die"

## Full Narrative

> Gurudev had the unique ability to heal not only physical and mental ailments but also address disturbances from the spirit world. His expertise extended to freeing individuals from the grip of spirit possession and attacks while also guiding these spirits towards their salvation.
>
> Virender ji recounts an episode involving his wife, who suffered from a severe gynaecological problem. Her condition had deteriorated significantly, rendering her unable to stand, leading doctors to recommend urgent surgery. It was at this juncture that a neighbour advised seeking Gurudev's help.
>
> Reflecting on their first attempt to meet Gurudev in Gurgaon during the Mahashivratri celebrations of 1984, he shares, "The throngs of devotees were daunting, and I hesitated to approach him. Thankfully, with the assistance of Inspector Bhajanlal Bishnoi, we managed a private audience with him a few weeks later. Upon meeting him, I felt an immediate sense of recognition. He welcomed me warmly, saying 'Aa gaya putt' as if he had been anticipating my visit. I poured out the saga of my wife's ailment, seeking his help."
>
> Despite their repeated visits to Gurudev, Virender ji's wife's suffering continued unabated. He remembers, "Once, frustrated on seeing her endure endless pain, I found myself pleading with Gurudev for a resolution—to either heal her completely or let her die. Upon hearing this, Gurudev called upon his disciple RP Sharma ji to 'remove her pain'. He disclosed that her torment was the work of a malevolent spirit but assured us, 'She will suffer no more'. Gurudev then encouraged her to stand, and as I watched, she not only stood up but also began to run. The once debilitating pain vanished, leading to her full recovery!"
> — Hingori and Virender ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.125–126
