# Virenderji's Uninvited Guests

## Aatma Sutra Version

> Virenderji, a retired judge in Haryana, was a devotee of Gurudev. One morning, having overslept, he missed going to the park for his morning walk. At 8:30 that morning, three of his friends came to his house. Apparently, he had invited them at 6:30 that morning at the park! This was very strange, since the judge had obviously missed his walk that morning. He did not know what to make of this incident. Here was a clear example of how another version of you can exist whilst on a path of spiritual evolution.
> — Hingori, *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.VI, p.71

## Witnessing Greatness Version (Ch.3) — The Full Account

*Witnessing Greatness* Ch.3 (pp.38–39) provides the complete testimony in Virender ji's own words, with two specific incidents and the mahaguru's direct confirmation:

> "In 1988, when I was stationed in Rohtak, Dr Jaising Kharap and his wife approached me one day with a curious inquiry. 'Where were you hurrying to this morning? We tried calling you, but you did not answer,' they said. Confused, I asked, 'At what time?' 'At 6 am,' they responded. I explained, 'I was praying at home at that time. It must have been someone else.' But they insisted, 'No, it was you. You walked past our gate while we were outside.' Despite their insistence, I dismissed the event."
>
> "Another incident happened after a month or so. Mr Bhan Singh Choudhary rang my doorbell one morning. When I opened the door, I said, 'Choudhary Saheb, how are you here?' He replied, puzzled, 'You invited me this morning for tea.' I asked, 'What time did we meet?' He said, 'It was about 6 am.' Days later, I went to Gurudev and informed him about these incidents. He said, 'I have already told you that I have created two of you.'"
> — Virender ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, p.38–39

## Context

The *Witnessing Greatness* version is the primary account. It establishes the specific mechanism behind the earlier *Aatma Sutra* summary: Gurudev had **actively created** Virender ji's nirman kaya — "one to work in the court and the other to assist me" — a promise made at their first meeting that Virender ji did not understand at the time. The two incidents are precise: the same time of day (6 am), two independent witnesses (Dr Jaising Kharap with his wife; Mr Bhan Singh Choudhary), in the same period of posting in Rohtak. Virender ji was praying at home in both cases. His first instinct in each case was to dismiss the event; Gurudev's confirmation — "I have already told you" — reframes both incidents as the fulfilment of a stated promise.

Virender ji is noted in the chapter as having his name, his brother Narender ji's name, and sister-in-law Saroj ji's name all recorded in the Bhrigu Samhita — anchoring their family's connection to the tradition thousands of years in advance.
