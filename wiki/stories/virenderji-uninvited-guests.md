# Virenderji's Uninvited Guests

## Full Narrative

*Aatma Sutra* brief account:

> Virenderji, a retired judge in Haryana, was a devotee of Gurudev. One morning, having overslept, he missed going to the park for his morning walk. At 8:30 that morning, three of his friends came to his house. Apparently, he had invited them at 6:30 that morning at the park! This was very strange, since the judge had obviously missed his walk that morning. He did not know what to make of this incident. Here was a clear example of how another version of you can exist whilst on a path of spiritual evolution.
> — Hingori, *Aatma Sutra*, Ch.VI, p.71

## Witnessing Greatness — Full Account (Ch.3)

*Witnessing Greatness* Ch.3 provides Virender ji's own first-person narration and identifies two specific incidents in Rohtak, 1988. The names of Virender ji (a distinguished judge), his brother Narender ji, and Narender ji's wife Saroj ji are recorded in the Bhrigu Samhita. Shortly after Virender ji's first meeting with Gurudev, the mahaguru promised:

> "I will create two of you — one to work in the court and the other to assist me."

At the time, Virender ji could not comprehend this.

### First Incident — The Kharaps

> "In 1988, when I was stationed in Rohtak, Dr Jaising Kharap and his wife approached me one day with a curious inquiry. 'Where were you hurrying to this morning? We tried calling you, but you did not answer,' they said. Confused, I asked, 'At what time?' 'At 6 am,' they responded. I explained, 'I was praying at home at that time. It must have been someone else'. But they insisted, 'No, it was you. You walked past our gate while we were outside'. Despite their insistence, I dismissed the event."

### Second Incident — Bhan Singh Choudhary

> "Another incident happened after a month or so. Mr Bhan Singh Choudhary rang my doorbell one morning. When I opened the door, I said, 'Choudhary Saheb, how are you here?' He replied, puzzled, 'You invited me this morning for tea'. I asked, 'What time did we meet?' He said, 'It was about 6 am'."

When Virender ji later reported both incidents to Gurudev, the mahaguru confirmed:

> "I have already told you that I have created two of you."

— *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.3, pp.36–38
