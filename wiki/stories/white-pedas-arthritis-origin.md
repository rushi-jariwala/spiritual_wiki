## The Broader Principle: Light-Coloured Foods and Subtle Energy

The chapter frames this personal account within a wider teaching on food and subtle energy. White and light-coloured foods are particularly receptive to environmental energies — both positive and negative. Food can be affected by the subtle energies present in its immediate environment, including the emotions and intentions of those who prepare or handle it.

The vulnerability of white pedas in Hingori's case illustrates an active, targeted form of this: the sweetmeats were charged through specific rituals and reverse mantras — a deliberately negative application of the same principle by which food can be energised positively.

### Practical Safeguard

To pre-empt the absorption of negative energy into white or light-coloured foods, Hingori recommends adding spices such as **turmeric or black pepper**. Doing so changes the colour of the dish, altering its receptivity profile and reducing its susceptibility to negative environmental influence. This is not a remedy after the fact but a preventive measure applied during preparation.

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.7, pp.88–89, 99.
