# Yash Sethi Ji — Dermatomyositis and the Healer from Nagpur

## Full Narrative

> My first encounter with the mahaguru was fuelled by my desperate search for relief from severe arthritis. I was motivated by the remarkable healing of a fellow Mumbaikar, Yash Sethi—a recovery that utterly astonished the medical community.
>
> Pradeep ji recounts his uncle Yash's miraculous story: "My uncle was grappling with a severe case of dermatomyositis, a muscle-weakening disease. The doctors had given up hope, telling us that his days were numbered.
>
> "My father spent endless hours praying in our home temple, seeking divine intervention. Our home became a sanctuary, visited by numerous saints and healers, but to no avail. My uncle's health continued to deteriorate."
>
> Hope arrived from an unexpected source. "My father received a call from his other brother who lived in Nagpur, who spoke of a man camped there, who worked with the Government of India and healed people with blessed water. Desperate for a cure, my father implored him to visit this healer."
>
> "When my uncle visited the camp, Gurudev initially refused to meet him and made him wait. However, the next day, Gurudev revealed that he already knew my uncle's purpose: seeking help for his ailing brother in Mumbai. Despite initially hesitating, he eventually agreed to come to Mumbai."
>
> Pradeep ji provides further insight, revealing, "I discovered later that Gurudev had already informed his colleagues a week in advance about their trip to Mumbai. He was possibly testing my uncle's resolve.
>
> "We welcomed Gurudev at the airport, and while I took an immediate liking to him, I remained sceptical about his healing powers," he admits. "Gurudev stayed with us for a week, during which he assured us of my uncle's recovery. He gave my uncle jal to drink and encouraged him to chant mantras as often as possible. Initially unable to move, my uncle began to show gradual improvement. Within six months, there was a significant improvement in his health."
>
> I recall visiting the Sethi residence to meet a healer treating Yash for his condition. I was seeking relief from arthritis, but the healer's efforts were fruitless. I saw Yash, completely paralysed and fading rapidly, his condition seemingly terminal. However, when I returned to their home a year later, I was met by a vibrant Yash, who had remarkably recovered from his dire state, though he walked with a slight limp. Stunned, I asked, "How are you okay?" His response was matter-of-fact: "Gurudev ne theek kiya hai (Gurudev has healed me)."
>
> Gurudev initiated seva in Mumbai at the Sethi residence, appointing Pradeep ji's father, Veer ji, as the sthan sanchalak.¹
>
> ¹ Individual responsible for managing and overseeing the activities at a sthan
> — Hingori and Pradeep ji, *Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, pp.117–118

## Source

*Witnessing Greatness*, Ch.9, "Sage of Solace," pp.117–118

> [!warning] Veer ji / Kulbir Sethi ji
> *The Guru of Gurus* Who's Who names **Kulbir Sethi ji (Papaji)** as Gurudev's first Mumbai disciple and head of the city's first sthan, and says Yash "recovered eighty per cent of his strength from stretcher to climbing steps." Ch.9 names Pradeep ji's father as **Veer ji**, the sthan sanchalak, and describes a slight limp. Likely the same person under a familiar name; not confirmed by the text.
