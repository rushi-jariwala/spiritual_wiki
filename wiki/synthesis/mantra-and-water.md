# Mantra and Water

*Date: 2026-09-28. Sources available: Karma Sutra, Aatma Sutra, Guru Sutra, The Guru of Gurus, Witnessing Greatness Ch.1–11.*

## The Pattern

Across three books, water is the element that keeps returning wherever mantras are practised — as the place to sit, the thing to drink, the vessel that holds the charge, and the sign that the mantra has taken hold. The Mahagayatri carries it most strongly, and carries fire alongside it.

| Where water appears | Source |
|---|---|
| **Asana** — the Mahagayatri is recited "immersed in water" / "in a body of water (bathtub)" | *Guru Sutra* Ch.3 ([[concepts/tantra]]); *The Guru of Gurus* ([[concepts/mantra-transfer]]) |
| **Jal by the asana** — a glass of water "magnetises any electromagnetic waves that escape from the auric layer" | *The Guru of Gurus* ([[concepts/mantra-transfer]]) |
| **Heat offset** — "Heat produced by specific mantras is offset by water intake" | *The Guru of Gurus* ([[concepts/mantra-transfer]]) |
| **Heat offset, first-hand** — Hingori's temper with Khemchand; "Regular hydration is advisable to mitigate the intense heat generated by this mantra" | *WG* Ch.11, pp.154–155 ([[stories/hingori-mahagayatri-fire-and-water]]) |
| **Water holds the mantra** — Mahashivratri jal energised by mantra; water "has its own consciousness"; sipped water carrying "10,000 recitations" | *The Guru of Gurus*; *WG* Ch.7 ([[concepts/mantra-transfer]]) |
| **Visions of water as siddhi** — a woman swimming underwater read by Gurudev as siddhi; Pooja Seth ji walking on water, snakes beneath | *WG* Ch.11, p.155 ([[concepts/mantra-vidya]]) |
| **"The essence of Gayatri is the life in water"** — hence, perhaps, Gurudev's advice against fish and seafood | Raji ji, *WG* Ch.11, p.155 ([[concepts/non-vegetarianism]]) |
| **Seafood advised against** on Ashish Khanna ji's first visit to the sthan; his guru appears across the table in Goa as he orders prawns | *WG* Ch.3, pp.39–40 ([[stories/ashish-khanna-goa-bilocation]]) |
| **Water drawn out of the land** — Mahagayatri and mustard seeds at Gopiganj | *WG* Ch.11, pp.155–156 ([[stories/ashish-khanna-gopiganj-water-mustard]]) |

## Fire Beside Water

The same mantra "ignites the fire element within practitioners and has a deep affinity with the water element" (*WG* Ch.11, p.154). *The Guru of Gurus* gives the fire side at its height: "The Mahagayatri mantra confers containment of fire" — Gurudev dipping his hands in boiling lemon tea every Mahashivratri ([[concepts/supernature]]). Ashish Khanna ji feels the contrast from the other side: switching to the Chamunda brought "a coolness and lightness… contrasting sharply with the heat that the Mahagayatri mantra had radiated" (*WG* Ch.11, p.152).

So the pattern is not water alone but a pairing: the mantra kindles heat in the practitioner, and water both balances that heat and signals, in dreams and visions, that the mantra has been mastered.

> [!tip] Asana and vision agree
> The Mahagayatri's prescribed asana (in water, *Guru Sutra* Ch.3) and the siddhi sign Gurudev read in Hingori's vision (a woman swimming underwater, *WG* Ch.11) point to the same element — the practice begins in water and its mastery is seen in water.

> [!question] Open
> Raji ji offers the fish-and-seafood link only as "Perhaps." Elsewhere, meat and fish are treated together as karmic debit ([[concepts/non-vegetarianism]]), not singled out for the Mahagayatri. *WG* Ch.12 (Elemental Symphony) is not yet ingested.
